# Authority and Power in Institutional Leadership

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Authority and Power in Institutional Leadership.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying authority, legitimacy, informal power, procedural fairness, information access, ethical credibility, accountability, voice, oversight, and governance risk as organizational and institutional conditions.

The scaffold treats authority and power as system-level governance constructs. It is not a tool for identifying dissenters, mapping political enemies, ranking individual influence, surveilling employees, or assessing worker loyalty. It supports institutional learning about how authority becomes legitimate, how informal power becomes visible enough to govern, and how organizations can align influence with accountability, fairness, and shared purpose.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- authority-system and governance review
- legitimacy and procedural-fairness analysis
- informal-power and information-flow review
- accountability and oversight diagnostics
- ethical-leadership and institutional-trust education
- decision-rights and governance-risk modeling
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee screening, employee ranking, individual influence scoring, loyalty scoring, psychological assessment, workplace surveillance, employment decision-making, dissent tracking, or worker risk labeling.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models authority effectiveness and governance risk as system-level constructs shaped by:

- formal legitimacy
- procedural fairness
- informational access
- practical influence
- ethical credibility
- accountability quality
- voice access
- oversight strength
- arbitrariness risk
- governance fragmentation
- distrust pressure
- informal power concentration
- shadow governance risk
- legitimacy decay risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, authority/governance-system review, legitimacy and procedural-fairness analysis, informal-power and information-flow review, ethical-leadership education, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, productivity-ranking tools, loyalty-scoring tools, political-influence scoring tools, dissent-tracking tools, psychological assessment tools, or automated personnel decision-making systems.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the authority system, governance system, decision process, information environment, accountability structure, leadership practice, organizational unit, oversight process, or institutional context—not the worth, loyalty, morality, productivity, influence, psychological status, or legitimacy of any individual worker.
