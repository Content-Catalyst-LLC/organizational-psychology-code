# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, authority/governance-system review, legitimacy and procedural-fairness analysis, informal-power and information-flow review, ethical-leadership education, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- loyalty scoring
- worker-risk scoring
- political-influence scoring
- dissent tracking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the authority system, governance system, decision process, information environment, accountability structure, leadership practice, organizational unit, oversight process, or institutional context.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as loyal, disloyal, politically powerful, politically risky, influential, noncompliant, dissenting, high-risk, low-risk, suitable, unsuitable, psychologically deficient, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Authority effectiveness and governance-risk signals may reflect structural contradictions, unclear decision rights, weak procedural fairness, poor information flow, low voice access, weak oversight, high arbitrariness, governance fragmentation, hidden influence, or legitimacy decay.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Authority and power analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear naming unfair process, hidden power, arbitrary decision-making, retaliation risk, governance fragmentation, or lived work experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible authority review.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
