# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and authority/governance review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Formal authority

- Are decision rights clear?
- Are responsibilities matched with authority?
- Are escalation paths and approval rights understandable?

## Procedural fairness

- Are decisions made through consistent and intelligible processes?
- Are affected groups given appropriate voice?
- Are decisions documented and explainable?

## Information flow

- Who controls access to information?
- What information reaches formal authorities?
- What problems remain invisible because of gatekeeping or fear?

## Informal power

- Where does influence operate outside the formal chart?
- Are informal networks improving coordination or bypassing accountability?
- Are bottlenecks or gatekeeping roles visible enough to govern?

## Accountability

- Can decisions be reviewed, challenged, corrected, or appealed?
- Are conflict-of-interest safeguards in place?
- Are high-status actors held to the same standards as others?

## Legitimacy and trust

- Do people experience authority as fair, competent, and ethically restrained?
- Are cynicism, silence, resistance, or workarounds signaling legitimacy decay?
- Is authority earning trust through repeated credible practice?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and governance review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, political-influence scorecard, dissent-tracking system, loyalty scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
