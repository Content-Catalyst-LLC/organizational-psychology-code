# Model Card: Synthetic Authority Effectiveness and Governance-Risk Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Authority Effectiveness, Legitimacy, Informal Power, and Governance-Risk Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, authority/governance-system review, legitimacy and procedural-fairness analysis, informal-power and information-flow review, ethical-leadership education, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- productivity ranking
- loyalty scoring
- political-influence scoring
- dissent tracking
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real leaders, real units, real workplaces, real boards, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- authority effectiveness scores
- governance-risk scores
- shadow-governance risk summaries
- legitimacy-decay risk summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as unit and institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker and stakeholder consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex authority, power, legitimacy, and governance phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure history, retaliation risk, informal power, hidden labor, governance politics, status hierarchy, and lived experience.
- Authority and power cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
