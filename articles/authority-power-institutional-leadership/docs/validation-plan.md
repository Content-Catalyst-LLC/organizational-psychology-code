# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, surveillance, dissent-tracking, political-influence scoring, productivity-ranking, loyalty-scoring, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent authority-system and institutional conditions rather than individual worker traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe governance, legitimacy, decision rights, fairness, accountability, voice, oversight, information flow, or institutional trust?
- Does it describe the authority environment rather than an individual's loyalty, influence, productivity, dissent, or psychological status?
- Could it be misused to identify critics, rank influence, punish resistance, or surveil informal relationships?
- Does the measure support institutional learning rather than political control?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- fear of retaliation
- silence and self-censorship
- hidden informal influence
- decision documentation quality
- meeting and agenda control
- access to information
- voice and appeal channels
- formal versus actual decision rights
- context behind numeric indicators

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match governance-review evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual monitoring, dissent tracking, political influence scoring, loyalty scoring, productivity ranking, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

An authority/governance review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- clearer decision rights
- stronger procedural fairness
- better information flow
- stronger accountability
- safer voice channels
- reduced arbitrariness
- reduced shadow governance
- better oversight
- restored institutional trust
