# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, decision-governance review, bias-risk analysis, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- executive ranking
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, decision process, governance structure, strategic review system, project review system, or institutional condition.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker or leader as biased, unbiased, rational, irrational, objective, subjective, competent, incompetent, high-risk, suitable, unsuitable, psychologically safe, psychologically unsafe, resilient, fragile, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Bias-risk signals may reflect structural underinvestment, weak decision governance, poor information flow, hierarchy suppression, time pressure, weak learning review, political filtering, distorted incentives, group conformity, or lack of protected dissent.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Bias analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear raising concerns, challenging assumptions, expressing uncertainty, or reporting decision risks, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible judgment.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation where appropriate
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
