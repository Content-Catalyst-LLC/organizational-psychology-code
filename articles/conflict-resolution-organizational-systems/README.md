# Conflict Resolution in Organizational Systems

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Conflict Resolution in Organizational Systems.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying conflict resolution as an institutional capability shaped by trust, communication clarity, psychological safety, procedural fairness, integrative problem-solving skill, blame intensity, power asymmetry, resource strain, mediation capacity, escalation dynamics, and collaborative resolution outcomes.

The scaffold treats conflict resolution not as personality management, individual conflict-proneness, forced harmony, or worker collaboration scoring. It treats conflict resolution as a team-level and organizational-system condition shaped by governance, fairness, voice, power, mediation pathways, negotiation quality, role clarity, and institutional learning.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- conflict-system diagnostics
- mediation and dispute-system education
- procedural fairness analysis
- constructive conflict capacity modeling
- collaborative resolution and escalation review
- power asymmetry and blame-culture review
- institutional learning and accountability review
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee screening, cultural-loyalty scoring, conflict-proneness scoring, collaboration scoring of workers, psychological assessment, productivity ranking, workplace surveillance, employment decision-making, or individual risk labeling.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models constructive conflict capacity and collaborative resolution as system-level constructs shaped by:

- trust level
- communication clarity
- psychological safety
- procedural fairness
- integrative problem-solving skill
- blame intensity
- power asymmetry
- resource strain
- mediation access
- escalation pressure
- collaborative resolution
- symbolic resolution risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, conflict-system review, mediation-process education, procedural fairness analysis, leadership and dispute-system analysis, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, cultural-loyalty scoring tools, conflict-proneness scoring tools, collaboration scoring tools, productivity-ranking tools, psychological assessment tools, or automated personnel decision-making systems.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the team environment, conflict system, dispute process, mediation pathway, organizational unit, governance structure, role system, institutional field, stakeholder relationship, or team-level conflict condition—not the worth, loyalty, cooperativeness, personality, employability, psychological status, morality, or legitimacy of any individual worker.

## Suggested workflow

1. Read `docs/ethics-scope-note.md`.
2. Review `docs/research-design.md`.
3. Inspect `docs/data-dictionary.md`.
4. Run the Python workflow in `python/conflict_resolution_model.py`.
