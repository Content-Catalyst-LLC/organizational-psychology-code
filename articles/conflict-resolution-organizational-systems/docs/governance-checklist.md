# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and conflict-system review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Conflict diagnosis

- Is the conflict task, process, relationship, structural, value-based, or power-related?
- Are repeated conflicts being treated as isolated interpersonal events?
- Has the organization identified whether role design, incentives, or resource strain are contributing?

## Procedural fairness

- Do parties understand the process?
- Are criteria transparent enough to be perceived as legitimate?
- Are outcomes explained and documented?

## Power asymmetry

- Can lower-power parties speak safely?
- Who controls framing, timing, documentation, and evidence?
- What protections exist after the process ends?

## Mediation access

- Are neutral resources available when direct dialogue is unsafe or insufficient?
- Are ombuds, mediation, grievance, and escalation pathways clear?
- Are processes matched to conflict severity and type?

## Blame intensity

- Does conflict trigger defensiveness, shame, or punishment?
- Are errors and disagreements examined for system causes?
- Does blame drive conflict underground?

## Collaborative resolution

- Are underlying interests identified?
- Are options expanded beyond initial positions?
- Are agreements implemented with clear responsibilities and follow-up?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and governance review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, collaboration scorecard, conflict-proneness scorecard, loyalty scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
