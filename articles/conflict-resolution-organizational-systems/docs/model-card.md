# Model Card: Synthetic Conflict Resolution and Collaborative Resolution Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Constructive Conflict Capacity, Escalation Risk, and Collaborative Resolution Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, conflict-system review, mediation-process education, procedural fairness analysis, leadership and dispute-system education, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- conflict-proneness scoring
- collaboration scoring of workers
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real conflicts, real mediation records, real teams, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- constructive conflict capacity scores
- conflict risk scores
- collaborative resolution predictions
- symbolic resolution-risk summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as team and institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker and stakeholder consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex conflict and mediation phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure power, history, retaliation risk, discrimination, trust, procedural legitimacy, workload, and lived experience.
- Conflict resolution cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
