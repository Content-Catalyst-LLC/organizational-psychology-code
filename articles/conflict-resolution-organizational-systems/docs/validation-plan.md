# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, surveillance, collaboration-scoring, conflict-proneness scoring, performance-ranking, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent conflict-system and institutional conditions rather than individual worker traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe a conflict system, team condition, or institutional process?
- Does it describe trust, fairness, communication clarity, mediation access, power asymmetry, blame intensity, or resource strain?
- Does it describe the environment rather than an individual's personality or conflict-proneness?
- Could it be misused to label or rank individuals?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- hidden conflict and underreporting
- fear of retaliation
- grievance-system access
- mediation confidentiality boundaries
- uneven procedural fairness across status groups
- aggregate scores hiding marginalized experience
- context behind numeric indicators
- recurrence of similar conflicts across units

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match conflict-system evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, loyalty scoring, collaboration scoring, conflict-proneness scoring, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

A conflict-system review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- stronger procedural fairness
- lower blame intensity
- safer conflict surfacing
- stronger mediation access
- clearer role and process design
- lower power asymmetry in conflict processes
- better documentation and follow-through
- reduced recurrence of avoidable conflicts
