# Culture Change in Organizations

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Culture Change in Organizations.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying culture change as an institutional process shaped by meaning, leadership credibility, reinforcement alignment, learning, psychological safety, identity adaptability, resistance, subcultural fragmentation, legacy lock-in, and legitimacy.

The scaffold treats culture change not as messaging, values language, or individual attitude compliance, but as a system-level process through which institutions revise shared assumptions, norms, rituals, incentives, memory, authority patterns, and the social credibility of stated values.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- culture-change capacity analysis
- organizational development research scaffolding
- institutional learning diagnostics
- culture-change governance review
- legitimacy and trust analysis
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee scoring, cultural-loyalty scoring, workplace surveillance, psychological assessment, productivity ranking, or employment decision-making.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models culture change capacity as a system-level construct shaped by:

- leadership credibility
- narrative coherence
- reinforcement alignment
- adaptive learning
- psychological safety
- identity adaptability
- resistance intensity
- subcultural fragmentation
- legacy lock-in
- leadership turnover
- successful cultural adoption
- symbolic culture change risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, culture-change review, organizational development education, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, cultural-loyalty scoring tools, productivity-ranking tools, or psychological assessment tools.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, cultural system, work system, change process, governance structure, subcultural field, or institutional condition—not the worth, loyalty, cultural fit, attitude, personality, employability, psychological status, or moral status of any individual worker.
