# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, culture-change review, organizational development education, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- cultural-fit scoring
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, cultural system, work system, change process, governance structure, subcultural field, or institutional condition.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as aligned, misaligned, loyal, disloyal, resistant, nonresistant, culturally fit, culturally unfit, high-risk, suitable, unsuitable, resilient, fragile, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Culture-change risk signals may reflect structural underinvestment, weak leadership credibility, misaligned incentives, low psychological safety, historical reform fatigue, uneven burden, power asymmetry, subcultural translation gaps, or legacy lock-in.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Culture analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear speaking honestly about culture, values, contradiction, leadership behavior, or lived experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible culture change.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
