# Model Card: Synthetic Culture Change Capacity Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Culture Change Capacity, Culture Risk, and Cultural Adoption Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, culture-change review, organizational development education, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- cultural-fit scoring
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real executives, real teams, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- culture change capacity scores
- culture risk scores
- successful cultural adoption predictions
- symbolic culture-change summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex cultural phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure power, history, memory, identity, and lived experience.
- Culture change cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
