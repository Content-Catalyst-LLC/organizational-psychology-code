# Validation Plan

For real organizational data:

1. Confirm data provenance and collection context.
2. Distinguish team-level, decision-level, individual-level, and organization-level variables.
3. Check missingness by team, period, function, and decision domain.
4. Evaluate whether missingness reflects fear, retaliation risk, low trust, or poor documentation.
5. Estimate reliability where multi-item measures exist.
6. Separate process quality from outcome quality.
7. Review metric validity and proxy distortion.
8. Protect psychological safety, dissent, and voice data.
9. Use qualitative review, interviews, and document analysis alongside models.
10. Use outputs for learning, not punishment.
