# Employee Motivation in Organizations

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Employee Motivation in Organizations.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying employee motivation as a system-level and institutional condition shaped by autonomy support, competence development, recognition quality, institutional trust, goal clarity, fairness perception, workload strain, control pressure, relatedness, meaningful work, psychological safety, and leadership credibility.

The scaffold treats motivation not as individual positivity, attitude compliance, productivity ranking, loyalty scoring, or psychological assessment. It treats motivational quality as an organizational condition shaped by work design, leadership, fairness, culture, incentives, workload, trust, belonging, and meaningful work.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- motivational-systems diagnostics
- work-design and autonomy-support review
- fairness, trust, and recognition analysis
- workload strain and control-pressure review
- culture, leadership, and psychological safety education
- motivation-quality and adaptive-performance simulation
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee screening, employee ranking, motivation scoring of workers, loyalty scoring, psychological assessment, workplace surveillance, employment decision-making, or individual worker risk labeling.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models motivation quality and motivational-system risk as system-level constructs shaped by:

- autonomy support
- competence development
- recognition quality
- institutional trust
- goal clarity
- fairness perception
- workload strain
- control pressure
- relatedness
- meaningful work
- psychological safety
- leadership credibility
- burnout accumulation
- adaptive performance
- motivation-system risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, motivational-systems review, work-design education, autonomy/fairness/trust/workload analysis, leadership and culture review, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, productivity-ranking tools, motivation-scoring tools, loyalty-scoring tools, psychological assessment tools, or automated personnel decision-making systems.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the motivational environment, work system, leadership system, organizational unit, feedback routine, work-design condition, fairness system, team environment, workload system, or institutional context—not the worth, productivity, loyalty, motivation, employability, morality, psychological status, or legitimacy of any individual worker.

## Suggested workflow

1. Read `docs/ethics-scope-note.md`.
2. Review `docs/research-design.md`.
3. Inspect `docs/data-dictionary.md`.
4. Run the Python workflow in `python/motivation_system_model.py`.
5. Run the R workflow in `r/motivation_system_model.R`.
6. Use `sql/schema.sql` for a reproducible relational representation.
7. Review `docs/validation-plan.md`, `docs/model-card.md`, and `docs/governance-checklist.md`.
8. Use the WordPress GitHub embed block in `github-embed-wordpress.html`.
