# Data Dictionary

## `data/raw/motivation_system_synthetic.csv`

This synthetic dataset describes organizational units across repeated motivational-system review periods. It is designed for methods demonstration only.

| Field | Description | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| `unit_id` | Synthetic organizational unit identifier | Categorical |
| `period` | Synthetic observation period | Integer |
| `autonomy_support` | Degree to which employees experience meaningful agency and discretion within clear expectations | 0–100 |
| `competence_development` | Degree to which work supports skill development, mastery, coaching, and growth | 0–100 |
| `recognition_quality` | Specificity, fairness, credibility, and usefulness of recognition | 0–100 |
| `institutional_trust` | Confidence that leadership and systems are credible, fair, and reliable | 0–100 |
| `goal_clarity` | Degree to which goals are understandable, meaningful, and actionable | 0–100 |
| `fairness_perception` | Perceived fairness of rewards, workload, evaluation, voice, and opportunity | 0–100 |
| `workload_strain` | Degree of overload, burnout pressure, unsustainable pace, or chronic depletion | 0–100 |
| `control_pressure` | Degree to which work is experienced as coercive, surveilled, or excessively externally regulated | 0–100 |
| `relatedness` | Degree of belonging, social connection, team support, and collective participation | 0–100 |
| `meaningful_work` | Degree to which employees experience work as purposeful, significant, or identity-connected | 0–100 |
| `psychological_safety` | Degree to which employees can raise concerns, ask questions, admit uncertainty, and learn without humiliation or retaliation | 0–100 |
| `leadership_credibility` | Degree to which leadership behavior is consistent, fair, transparent, and trust-building | 0–100 |
| `performance_risk` | Synthetic binary indicator of elevated performance-system risk | 0/1 |
| `burnout_risk` | Synthetic binary indicator of elevated exhaustion or depletion risk | 0/1 |
| `controlled_motivation_risk` | Synthetic binary indicator of motivation driven primarily by pressure, fear, or external control | 0/1 |

## Interpretation

Variables are unit-level, motivational-system-level, and institutional indicators. They should not be interpreted as measures of individual productivity, effort, loyalty, motivation, employability, morality, psychological status, or worth.
