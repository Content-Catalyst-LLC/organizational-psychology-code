# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, motivational-systems review, work-design education, autonomy/fairness/trust/workload analysis, leadership and culture review, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- motivation scoring
- worker-risk scoring
- loyalty scoring
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the motivational environment, work system, leadership system, organizational unit, feedback routine, work-design condition, fairness system, team environment, workload system, or institutional context.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as motivated, unmotivated, productive, unproductive, loyal, disloyal, high-risk, low-risk, suitable, unsuitable, psychologically deficient, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Motivation quality and performance-risk signals may reflect structural contradictions, unclear authority, weak autonomy, poor development opportunity, low trust, unfair reward systems, weak psychological safety, unsustainable workload, excessive control pressure, or leadership credibility problems.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Motivation analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear naming low trust, unfairness, exhaustion, lack of autonomy, control pressure, or lived work experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible motivational-system review.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
