# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and motivational-system review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Autonomy

- Do employees have meaningful discretion within clear expectations?
- Are roles over-controlled or needlessly surveilled?
- Can people adapt methods to the realities of the work?

## Competence

- Do employees receive training, feedback, coaching, and development access?
- Are goals matched with skill and support?
- Is challenge developmental rather than humiliating?

## Recognition

- Is recognition specific, fair, and connected to real contribution?
- Does recognition include hidden labor, mentoring, coordination, and repair work?
- Does recognition reinforce visible performance only?

## Trust and fairness

- Are leadership decisions credible and explainable?
- Are rewards, workload, voice, and development opportunities fair?
- Can employees trust that effort will not be exploited?

## Workload and control pressure

- Is motivation being sustained or consumed by exhaustion?
- Are high-effort employees carrying hidden overload?
- Does the organization confuse fear-driven compliance with motivation?

## Meaning and relatedness

- Do employees understand why the work matters?
- Does the team environment support belonging and cooperation?
- Is purpose being used responsibly, or exploited to justify strain?

## Psychological safety

- Can people raise concerns, name uncertainty, and report problems without retaliation?
- Are low-trust signals treated as system evidence rather than employee negativity?
- Does feedback support learning or self-protection?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and governance review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, motivation scorecard, loyalty scorecard, productivity scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
