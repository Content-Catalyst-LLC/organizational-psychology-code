# Model Card: Synthetic Motivation Quality and Adaptive-Performance Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Motivation Quality, Work Design, and Adaptive-Performance Risk Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, motivational-systems review, work-design education, autonomy/fairness/trust/workload analysis, leadership and culture review, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- productivity ranking
- motivation scoring
- loyalty scoring
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real units, real workplaces, real leaders, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- motivation quality scores
- motivational-system risk scores
- adaptive-performance risk summaries
- burnout risk summaries
- controlled-motivation risk summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as unit and institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker and stakeholder consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex motivational and work-design phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure power, history, retaliation risk, hidden labor, qualitative work, resource constraints, workload, and lived experience.
- Motivation cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
