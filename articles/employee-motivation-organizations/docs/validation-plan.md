# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, surveillance, productivity-ranking, motivation-scoring, performance-ranking, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent motivational-system and institutional conditions rather than individual worker traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe work design, leadership, autonomy, trust, fairness, workload, recognition, psychological safety, meaning, or team conditions?
- Does it describe the motivational environment rather than an individual worker's worth, loyalty, motivation, character, productivity, or psychological status?
- Could it be misused to label, rank, or discipline individuals?
- Does the measure support institutional learning rather than individual blame?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- fear of retaliation
- survey nonresponse and self-censorship
- aggregate scores hiding unequal conditions
- hidden labor and unmeasured contribution
- workload intensity and staffing
- leadership and supervision differences
- access to development and advancement
- psychological safety differences
- context behind numeric indicators

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match motivational-system review evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, motivation scoring, productivity ranking, loyalty scoring, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

A motivational-system review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- stronger autonomy support
- better competence development
- more credible recognition
- stronger institutional trust
- clearer goals
- fairer processes
- reduced workload strain
- lower control pressure
- stronger psychological safety
- better leadership credibility
