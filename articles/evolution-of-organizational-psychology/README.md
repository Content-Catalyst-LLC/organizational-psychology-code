# The Evolution of Organizational Psychology

This companion scaffold supports the article **“The Evolution of Organizational Psychology.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying the historical development of organizational psychology as a changing field of research emphases, methods, institutional pressures, and ethical responsibilities.

The scaffold models the field’s movement from early industrial concerns with efficiency, selection, and measurement toward a broader systems-oriented science of motivation, teams, culture, leadership, fairness, well-being, digital work, people analytics, and institutional accountability.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology history education
- synthetic-data methods demonstration
- research-emphasis mapping
- history-of-field visualization
- descriptive modeling of conceptual change
- responsible progress and ethics-oriented analysis
- reproducible workflows in R, Python, SQL, Julia, C, C++, Fortran, Go, and Rust
- teaching how fields evolve from narrow administrative utility toward broader institutional responsibility

It is not designed for ranking scholars, ranking departments, ranking journals, evaluating real research programs, assessing employees, screening candidates, monitoring workers, tracking dissent, or automating personnel decisions.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow represents organizational psychology’s historical evolution across several emphases:

- selection and testing
- efficiency and work design
- motivation and job attitudes
- groups and teams
- culture, leadership, and change
- well-being and occupational stress
- justice, validity, and ethics
- digital work and people analytics
- systems and institutional accountability

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, organizational psychology education, history-of-field analysis, research-emphasis mapping, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, productivity-ranking tools, loyalty-scoring tools, dissent-tracking tools, psychological assessment tools, scholar-ranking tools, department-ranking tools, journal-ranking tools, or automated personnel decision-making systems.
