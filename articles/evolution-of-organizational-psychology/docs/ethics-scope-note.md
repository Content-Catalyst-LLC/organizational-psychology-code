# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, organizational psychology education, history-of-field analysis, research-emphasis mapping, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- loyalty scoring
- dissent tracking
- psychological assessment
- scholar ranking
- department ranking
- journal ranking
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the synthetic historical period, conceptual tradition, field-level emphasis, research orientation, or teaching scenario.

The workflow should never be used to evaluate the worth, productivity, legitimacy, status, influence, employability, or intellectual value of any real person, worker, scholar, department, journal, organization, or institution.

## Responsible interpretation

The included data are synthetic. They are useful for demonstrating how a field can be modeled as a changing distribution of emphases. They are not archival facts or bibliometric findings.
