# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for teaching and responsible interpretation.

## Synthetic data clarity

- Are all charts and tables clearly labeled as synthetic?
- Is the dataset described as a conceptual demonstration rather than historical measurement?
- Are users warned against interpreting scores as publication counts or rankings?

## Responsible interpretation

- Does the workflow avoid ranking scholars, departments, journals, workers, employees, or institutions?
- Are index weights transparent and editable?
- Are limitations clearly documented?

## Field-history caution

- Does the analysis distinguish interpretation from archival evidence?
- Does it avoid implying that one historical phase fully replaced another?
- Does it show that selection, measurement, and efficiency remain part of the field while later concerns expanded the field?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports organizational psychology education and methods demonstration. It is not a ranking, surveillance, employment, or psychological assessment framework.
