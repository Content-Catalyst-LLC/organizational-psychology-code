# Method Notes

This scaffold demonstrates a transparent way to model the evolution of organizational psychology as a changing field orientation.

## Orientation indices

The workflows calculate:

- `industrial_orientation`: weighted emphasis on selection, testing, efficiency, work design, and motivation
- `organizational_orientation`: weighted emphasis on motivation, teams, culture, leadership, well-being, justice, and digital work
- `systems_ethics_orientation`: weighted emphasis on culture, leadership, well-being, justice, digital work, teams, and institutional accountability
- `field_expansion_index`: difference between organizational orientation and industrial orientation
- `responsible_progress_index`: emphasis on validity, well-being, justice, learning, and digital governance

## Methodological caution

Synthetic indices are useful for teaching conceptual change, but they should not be treated as historical measurements. Real history-of-field research would require archival work, historiography, bibliometric analysis, professional society records, journal data, and qualitative interpretation.
