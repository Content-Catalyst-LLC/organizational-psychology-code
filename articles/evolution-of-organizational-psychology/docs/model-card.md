# Model Card: Synthetic Organizational Psychology Field-Evolution Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Organizational Psychology Field-Evolution Model

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, organizational psychology history education, field-emphasis mapping, and reproducible examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- productivity ranking
- loyalty scoring
- dissent tracking
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment
- scholar ranking
- department ranking
- journal ranking

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real publication counts, citation records, occupational records, workers, scholars, departments, journals, professional societies, or institutions.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- field-orientation indices
- responsible-progress indices
- historical phase summaries
- synthetic trend tables
- synthetic visualizations
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as educational and conceptual artifacts, not historical measurement or evaluation.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate historical claims.
- Weighting choices are interpretive.
- Real historiography requires archival, bibliometric, and qualitative evidence.
- Simplified categories may obscure diversity within the field.
