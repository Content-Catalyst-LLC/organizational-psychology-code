# Research Design

This scaffold supports methods demonstration for studying the evolution of organizational psychology as a changing distribution of field emphases.

## Research question

How can the history of organizational psychology be represented as a synthetic shift from early industrial emphases on efficiency, selection, and measurement toward broader organizational emphases on motivation, teams, culture, leadership, justice, well-being, digital work, and institutional accountability?

## Conceptual assumptions

1. Organizational psychology evolved by expansion, not simple replacement.
2. Selection, testing, and efficiency remain part of the field but are no longer the whole field.
3. Motivation, job attitudes, groups, teams, culture, leadership, change, well-being, justice, and digital work widened the field’s scope.
4. Responsible progress depends on validity, human well-being, justice, learning, and governance.
5. Synthetic field-emphasis data are educational artifacts, not bibliometric findings.

## Synthetic modeling aims

The scaffold demonstrates how to:

- represent historical phases as synthetic field-emphasis scores
- calculate industrial orientation and organizational orientation indices
- calculate systems-ethics and responsible-progress indices
- visualize changing emphases over time
- compare conceptual scenarios
- document responsible-use constraints

## Appropriate interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as teaching and methods demonstrations. They should not be read as measured publication counts, real historical estimates, bibliometric claims, rankings of scholars, or evaluations of departments, journals, programs, or institutions.
