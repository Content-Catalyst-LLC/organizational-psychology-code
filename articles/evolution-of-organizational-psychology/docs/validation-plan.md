# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration only.

## 1. Construct validation

Check whether each field-emphasis construct has a clear conceptual meaning and whether the weighting scheme reflects the article’s historical argument.

## 2. Historical plausibility review

Review whether the synthetic pattern broadly matches the interpretive arc of the field:

- early selection and efficiency emphasis
- human relations and morale expansion
- motivation and job-attitude growth
- culture, leadership, teams, and organizational development expansion
- well-being, justice, digital work, and people analytics growth

## 3. Model validation

Assess whether the workflows are transparent, reproducible, and easy to modify. Synthetic outputs should be treated as teaching artifacts.

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for ranking scholars, departments, journals, workers, employees, or institutions.

## 5. Interpretation validation

Any chart or table must include a note that scores are synthetic and conceptual, not measured historical data.
