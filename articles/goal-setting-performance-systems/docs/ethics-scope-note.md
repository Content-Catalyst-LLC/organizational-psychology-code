# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, goal-system review, performance-system education, feedback-quality analysis, metric-governance review, strategic-alignment analysis, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- goal-compliance scoring
- worker-risk scoring
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the goal system, performance system, organizational unit, feedback routine, metric-governance process, strategy-translation system, incentive design, team environment, work system, or institutional context.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as productive, unproductive, compliant, noncompliant, motivated, unmotivated, loyal, disloyal, high-risk, low-risk, suitable, unsuitable, psychologically deficient, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Goal-system and performance-risk signals may reflect structural contradictions, unclear strategy, poor translation, weak feedback quality, unrealistic targets, inadequate authority, insufficient resources, workload strain, proxy drift, metric distortion, or incentive misalignment.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Goal-system analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear naming unrealistic goals, distorted metrics, resource constraints, poor feedback, workload strain, gaming pressure, or lived work experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible performance-system review.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
