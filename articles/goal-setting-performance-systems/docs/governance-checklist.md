# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and goal-system review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Goal clarity

- Are goals specific, understandable, and behaviorally actionable?
- Do employees understand tradeoffs and priorities?
- Are goals clear at every level of translation?

## Challenge and feasibility

- Are targets ambitious but credible?
- Do people have the time, staffing, authority, tools, and resources required?
- Are goals calibrated to task complexity?

## Feedback quality

- Is feedback timely, specific, fair, and developmental?
- Can employees explain constraints and system barriers?
- Does monitoring support learning rather than concealment?

## Strategic alignment

- Do goals connect visibly to institutional priorities?
- Are local goals reinforcing or undermining system-level outcomes?
- Does goal cascade preserve purpose or merely transmit numbers?

## Metric governance

- What value is each metric intended to represent?
- How could rational actors game the metric?
- What qualitative evidence should accompany the indicator?
- Are metrics producing proxy drift?

## Incentive alignment

- Do incentives reward the behavior the organization actually wants?
- Are rewards encouraging short-termism, siloing, quality neglect, or hidden harm?
- Are cooperative and long-term contributions recognized?

## Overload and strain

- Are goal systems creating unrealistic target pressure?
- Are employees reporting burnout, gaming pressure, or ethical tension?
- Are missed goals examined systemically before blame is assigned?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and governance review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, productivity scorecard, goal-compliance scorecard, loyalty scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
