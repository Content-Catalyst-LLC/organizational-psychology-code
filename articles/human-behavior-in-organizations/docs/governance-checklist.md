# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and organizational behavior review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Behavior and work design

- Are roles, goals, responsibilities, and decision rights clear?
- Are workload and staffing sustainable?
- Are incentives aligned with ethical, cooperative, and learning-oriented behavior?

## Motivation and behavior

- Are people supported through meaning, fairness, recognition, autonomy, and resources?
- Are low motivation or disengagement being interpreted in context?
- Are hidden labor and emotional labor recognized?

## Leadership and culture

- Is leadership trusted?
- Are cultural norms enacted through behavior rather than slogans?
- Do leaders protect voice and respond constructively to concerns?

## Voice and power

- Can people raise concerns without fear?
- Are lower-status voices protected?
- Is silence being mistaken for agreement?
- Are anti-retaliation safeguards real and trusted?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and responsible organizational development. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, loyalty scorecard, dissent-tracking system, productivity scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
