# Method Notes

This scaffold demonstrates a systems-oriented approach to human behavior in organizations.

## Conceptual model

Behavior quality is modeled as a function of capability, motivation, role clarity, psychological safety, leadership trust, cultural norm strength, incentive alignment, workload pressure, burnout pressure, and hierarchical pressure.

Voice behavior is modeled as a function of psychological safety, leadership trust, perceived voice efficacy, fear of retaliation, and hierarchical pressure.

## Suggested methods

- descriptive unit summaries
- multilevel modeling
- logistic modeling of voice behavior
- scenario analysis
- behavior-system risk review
- psychological safety and leadership trust review
- workload, burnout, fear, and hierarchy review
- qualitative validation

## Methodological caution

Synthetic data cannot validate real-world conclusions. Real organizational psychology research requires careful design, ethical review, validated measures, attention to power and privacy, anti-retaliation protections, and interpretive humility.
