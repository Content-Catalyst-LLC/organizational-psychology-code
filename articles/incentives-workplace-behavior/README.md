# Incentives and Workplace Behavior

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Incentives and Workplace Behavior.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying incentive systems as institutional, cultural, psychological, and ethical systems shaped by expectancy strength, fairness perception, strategic alignment, intrinsic support, feedback clarity, distortion risk, overload pressure, ethical risk, cooperation support, recognition legitimacy, and reward-system governance.

The scaffold treats incentives not as individual productivity scoring, worker ranking, loyalty scoring, reward-compliance scoring, or automated personnel assessment. It treats them as organizational systems for translating strategy into behavior, rewarding contribution, shaping culture, protecting fairness, identifying metric distortion, supporting cooperation, and preserving ethical judgment under conditions of complexity.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- incentive-system diagnostics
- reward-governance education
- fairness and legitimacy review
- metric distortion and proxy-failure analysis
- cooperation and team-incentive review
- ethical-risk and overload analysis
- recognition-pattern and hidden-labor review
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee screening, employee ranking, individual productivity scoring, incentive-compliance scoring, cultural-loyalty scoring, psychological assessment, workplace surveillance, employment decision-making, or individual worker risk labeling.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models incentive effectiveness and performance-system risk as system-level constructs shaped by:

- expectancy strength
- fairness perception
- strategic alignment
- intrinsic support
- feedback clarity
- distortion risk
- overload pressure
- ethical risk
- cooperation support
- recognition legitimacy
- reward transparency
- hidden labor recognition
- incentive-gaming risk
- symbolic reward risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, incentive-system review, reward-governance education, fairness and metric-distortion analysis, ethical-risk review, cooperation and culture review, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, productivity-ranking tools, incentive-compliance scoring tools, cultural-loyalty scoring tools, psychological assessment tools, or automated personnel decision-making systems.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the incentive system, reward-governance process, performance system, organizational unit, feedback routine, metric-governance process, recognition system, promotion pathway, team environment, work system, or institutional context—not the worth, productivity, loyalty, motivation, compliance, employability, morality, psychological status, or legitimacy of any individual worker.

## Suggested workflow

1. Read `docs/ethics-scope-note.md`.
2. Review `docs/research-design.md`.
3. Inspect `docs/data-dictionary.md`.
4. Run the Python workflow in `python/incentive_system_model.py`.
5. Run the R workflow in `r/incentive_system_model.R`.
6. Use `sql/schema.sql` for a reproducible relational representation.
7. Review `docs/validation-plan.md`, `docs/model-card.md`, and `docs/governance-checklist.md`.
8. Use the WordPress GitHub embed block in `github-embed-wordpress.html`.
