# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, incentive-system review, reward-governance education, fairness and metric-distortion analysis, ethical-risk review, cooperation and culture review, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- incentive-compliance scoring
- worker-risk scoring
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the incentive system, reward-governance process, performance system, organizational unit, feedback routine, metric-governance process, recognition system, promotion pathway, team environment, work system, or institutional context.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as productive, unproductive, compliant, noncompliant, motivated, unmotivated, loyal, disloyal, high-risk, low-risk, suitable, unsuitable, psychologically deficient, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Incentive-system and performance-risk signals may reflect structural contradictions, unclear reward criteria, poor feedback quality, unfair distribution, hidden labor, weak cooperation incentives, overloaded targets, metric distortion, proxy failure, ethical pressure, or incentive misalignment.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Incentive-system analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear naming unfair rewards, distorted metrics, overloaded targets, hidden labor, ethical pressure, gaming pathways, or lived work experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible reward-governance review.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
