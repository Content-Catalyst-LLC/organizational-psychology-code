# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and incentive-system review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Expectancy and attainability

- Can employees realistically influence the rewarded outcome?
- Are performance expectations matched to authority, resources, staffing, and time?
- Are rewards tied to conditions employees can reasonably affect?

## Fairness and legitimacy

- Are rewards distributed through transparent and legitimate criteria?
- Are comparison groups, role differences, and structural constraints considered?
- Can employees understand and challenge reward decisions without retaliation?

## Strategic alignment

- Do incentives reinforce the institution’s stated values and long-term purpose?
- Do rewards support quality, cooperation, ethics, and learning?
- Are incentives aligned across interdependent teams and units?

## Intrinsic motivation

- Do incentives support autonomy, mastery, meaning, and professional identity?
- Could external rewards crowd out intrinsic motivation?
- Are mission and purpose being used to justify under-reward or overwork?

## Metric governance

- What value is each rewarded metric intended to represent?
- How could rational actors game the metric?
- What qualitative evidence should accompany the indicator?
- Are incentives producing proxy failure?

## Cooperation and hidden labor

- Are mentoring, coordination, translation, repair, onboarding, and support work recognized?
- Do individual rewards damage team performance?
- Are shared incentives appropriate for interdependent work?

## Ethical risk

- Could the incentive make harmful shortcuts strategically rational?
- Are quality, safety, stakeholder trust, and compliance protected?
- Can employees safely report distorted or dangerous incentives?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and governance review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, productivity scorecard, incentive-compliance scorecard, loyalty scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
