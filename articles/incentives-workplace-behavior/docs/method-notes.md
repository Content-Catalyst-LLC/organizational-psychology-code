# Method Notes

This scaffold demonstrates how incentive effectiveness, performance risk, incentive-gaming risk, and symbolic reward risk can be modeled while preserving interpretive caution.

## Conceptual model

The model treats incentive effectiveness as an interaction among:

- expectancy strength
- fairness perception
- strategic alignment
- intrinsic support
- feedback clarity
- cooperation support
- recognition legitimacy
- reward transparency
- hidden-labor recognition
- distortion risk
- overload pressure
- ethical risk

## Suggested methods

- descriptive diagnostics
- unit-level incentive-system dashboards
- logistic regression for performance risk
- incentive gaming-risk review
- symbolic reward-risk review
- scenario analysis
- sensitivity analysis
- qualitative validation
- incentive-plan audit
- compensation and promotion review
- recognition-pattern review
- hidden-labor analysis
- ethics and stakeholder-impact review

## Methodological caution

Incentive systems are partly measurable but not fully captured by metrics. They involve meaning, fairness, trust, strategy, reward transparency, professional identity, teamwork, culture, power, hidden labor, workload, and lived experience. Quantitative outputs should be paired with qualitative research and ethical governance.
