# Model Card: Synthetic Incentive Effectiveness and Performance-Risk Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Incentive Effectiveness, Reward Governance, and Performance-System Risk Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, incentive-system review, reward-governance education, fairness and metric-distortion analysis, ethical-risk review, cooperation and culture review, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- productivity ranking
- incentive-compliance scoring
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real units, real workplaces, real leaders, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- incentive effectiveness scores
- performance-system risk scores
- incentive-gaming risk summaries
- symbolic reward-risk summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as unit and institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker and stakeholder consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex incentive-system and reward-governance phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure power, history, retaliation risk, hidden labor, qualitative work, resource constraints, workload, and lived experience.
- Incentive systems cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
