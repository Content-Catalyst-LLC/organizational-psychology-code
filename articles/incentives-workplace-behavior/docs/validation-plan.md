# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, surveillance, productivity-ranking, incentive-compliance scoring, performance-ranking, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent incentive-system and institutional conditions rather than individual worker traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe a reward system, recognition process, incentive plan, feedback process, fairness condition, or institutional system?
- Does it describe expectancy, fairness, alignment, intrinsic support, feedback clarity, cooperation support, recognition legitimacy, reward transparency, distortion risk, overload, ethical risk, or hidden-labor recognition?
- Does it describe the environment rather than an individual's productivity, loyalty, effort, motivation, or incentive compliance?
- Could it be misused to label, rank, or discipline individuals?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- fear of retaliation
- underreporting of distorted incentives
- dashboard and metric validity
- aggregate scores hiding unit-level constraints
- hidden labor and unmeasured work
- reward and promotion opacity
- incentive-plan eligibility
- cross-functional dependency conditions
- ethics and compliance conditions
- context behind numeric indicators

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match incentive-system review evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, productivity ranking, incentive-compliance scoring, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

An incentive-system review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- fairer rewards
- clearer criteria
- stronger strategic alignment
- reduced overload pressure
- better metric governance
- lower ethical risk
- stronger recognition of hidden labor
- improved team and cooperation incentives
- better feedback and review routines
