# Information Flow and Organizational Communication

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Information Flow and Organizational Communication.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying information flow as an institutional condition of organizational knowledge, communication, decision quality, learning, and resilience.

The scaffold treats information flow not as raw message volume, platform activity, or individual responsiveness, but as a system-level property shaped by signal quality, timeliness, interpretive clarity, cross-functional connectivity, psychological safety, distortion risk, overload pressure, hierarchy delay, external signal turbulence, and decision consequences.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- organizational communication research scaffolding
- communication-system review
- knowledge-flow governance
- institutional learning diagnostics
- decision-quality and escalation-risk analysis
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee scoring, workplace surveillance, message monitoring, psychological assessment, productivity ranking, or employment decision-making.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models information flow quality as a system-level construct shaped by:

- signal quality
- timeliness
- interpretive clarity
- cross-functional connectivity
- psychological safety
- distortion risk
- overload pressure
- hierarchy delay
- external signal turbulence
- decision error risk
- communication risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, organizational communication review, knowledge-flow governance, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, communication-monitoring systems, productivity-ranking systems, or psychological assessment tools.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, work system, communication process, decision system, knowledge-flow architecture, governance structure, or institutional condition—not the worth, responsiveness, communication style, competence, employability, psychological status, or productivity of an individual worker.

## Suggested workflow

1. Read `docs/ethics-scope-note.md`.
2. Review `docs/research-design.md`.
