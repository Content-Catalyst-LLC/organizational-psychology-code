# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, values-governance review, organizational ethics education, legitimacy analysis, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- cultural-fit scoring
- morality scoring
- values-alignment scoring of workers
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, values system, governance structure, institutional field, stakeholder relationship, organizational unit, cultural system, behavioral-expectation system, or legitimacy condition.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as aligned, misaligned, ethical, unethical, loyal, disloyal, morally credible, morally suspect, culturally fit, culturally unfit, resistant, nonresistant, high-risk, suitable, unsuitable, resilient, fragile, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Values-governance risk signals may reflect structural contradictions, unclear behavioral expectations, weak leadership credibility, misaligned incentives, low psychological safety, selective accountability, hierarchy exemption, stakeholder mistrust, legitimacy pressure, field-level pressure, or subcultural fragmentation.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Values analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear speaking honestly about values, contradiction, leadership behavior, accountability, ethics, or lived institutional experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible values governance.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
