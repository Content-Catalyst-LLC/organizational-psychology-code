# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and values-governance review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, or assess individual workers.

## Values clarity

- Are values translated into behavioral expectations?
- Are expectations role-sensitive without becoming status-exempt?
- Are value violations defined clearly enough for review?

## Leadership credibility

- Do leadership decisions match values language?
- Are crises handled in ways that preserve declared commitments?
- Are resource decisions consistent with stated priorities?

## Reinforcement alignment

- Do promotions, recognition, sanctions, budgets, and performance systems reinforce values?
- Are contrary behaviors rewarded informally?
- Are values supported with time, staffing, authority, and training?

## Psychological safety

- Can members name value-practice gaps without retaliation?
- Are dissent and concern reporting treated as institutional learning?
- Is silence interpreted cautiously rather than as agreement?

## Accountability

- Are powerful actors held to the same or higher standards?
- Are repairs tracked after value violations?
- Are accountability processes transparent and credible?

## Decoupling risk

- Where do formal commitments diverge from lived practice?
- Which value claims are ceremonial?
- Which stakeholders perceive a gap between institutional language and behavior?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and governance review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, cultural-fit screen, values-alignment scorecard, or morality rating framework.
