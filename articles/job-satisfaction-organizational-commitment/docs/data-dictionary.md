# Data Dictionary

## `data/raw/satisfaction_commitment_synthetic.csv`

This synthetic dataset describes organizational units across repeated work-attitude review periods. It is designed for methods demonstration only.

| Field | Description | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| `unit_id` | Synthetic organizational unit identifier | Categorical |
| `period` | Synthetic observation period | Integer |
| `leadership_support` | Degree to which leadership provides clarity, fairness, recognition, development, and workload protection | 0–100 |
| `reward_fairness` | Perceived fairness of compensation, recognition, advancement, benefits, and reward distribution | 0–100 |
| `developmental_opportunity` | Credible access to growth, learning, mobility, mentoring, and future opportunity | 0–100 |
| `workload_balance` | Sustainability of workload, staffing, pace, and work-life demands | 0–100 |
| `institutional_trust` | Confidence that procedures, leaders, and systems are fair, consistent, and credible | 0–100 |
| `cultural_alignment` | Degree to which lived culture and values are experienced as meaningful and coherent | 0–100 |
| `role_insecurity` | Uncertainty, instability, or insecurity around role, future, or employment conditions | 0–100 |
| `exhaustion_pressure` | Chronic strain, depletion, burnout pressure, overload, or unsustainable demand | 0–100 |
| `affective_commitment` | Emotional attachment, identification, and desire to remain | 0–100 |
| `continuance_commitment` | Perceived cost or constraint associated with leaving | 0–100 |
| `normative_commitment` | Sense of obligation, duty, or reciprocal loyalty | 0–100 |
| `high_retention_risk` | Synthetic binary indicator of elevated retention risk at the unit level | 0/1 |
| `symbolic_commitment_risk` | Synthetic binary indicator of retention or commitment language without healthy attachment conditions | 0/1 |

## Interpretation

Variables are unit-level, work-system-level, and institutional indicators. They should not be interpreted as measures of individual loyalty, positivity, commitment, psychological status, morality, productivity, employability, or worth.
