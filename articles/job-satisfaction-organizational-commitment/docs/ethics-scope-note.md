# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, work-attitude review, retention-system education, fairness and workload analysis, leadership and culture review, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- commitment scoring of workers
- retention-risk scoring of individual workers
- worker-risk scoring
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the work system, organizational unit, leadership system, fairness system, workload system, role design, institutional trust condition, culture, retention environment, or organization-level attachment condition.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as loyal, disloyal, committed, uncommitted, positive, negative, engaged, disengaged, high-risk, low-risk, suitable, unsuitable, productive, unproductive, psychologically deficient, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Satisfaction, commitment, and retention-risk signals may reflect structural contradictions, unclear authority, unfair reward systems, weak leadership support, poor development opportunity, workload strain, institutional distrust, role insecurity, exhaustion pressure, or constrained labor-market mobility.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Work-attitude analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear naming dissatisfaction, distrust, exhaustion, unfairness, leadership problems, workload stress, or lived work experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible work-attitude review.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
