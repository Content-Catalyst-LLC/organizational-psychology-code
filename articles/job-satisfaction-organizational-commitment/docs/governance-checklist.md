# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and work-attitude review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Job satisfaction

- Is work meaningful, coherent, and supported?
- Are workload, autonomy, feedback, recognition, and role clarity credible?
- Are dissatisfaction signals treated as system evidence rather than negativity?

## Affective commitment

- Do employees want to remain because they identify with credible work, culture, and purpose?
- Does the organization deserve attachment through fairness and trust?
- Are values enacted or merely stated?

## Continuance commitment

- Are people staying because leaving would be too costly?
- Is retention healthy attachment or constrained dependence?
- Are benefits, mobility, economic pressure, or role insecurity shaping retention?

## Normative commitment

- Is obligation grounded in real reciprocity?
- Are employees pressured to remain under unfair or unsustainable conditions?
- Is loyalty being used to normalize overwork?

## Fairness and trust

- Are reward, promotion, recognition, workload, and development systems fair?
- Are decisions explained with credible criteria?
- Do employees trust leadership and institutional process?

## Workload and exhaustion

- Is commitment being consumed by chronic overload?
- Are high-commitment workers carrying disproportionate hidden labor?
- Are staffing and workload assumptions sustainable?

## Retention

- Are employees staying for healthy reasons?
- Are turnover patterns concentrated by unit, role, identity, career stage, or supervisor?
- What institutional memory is at risk?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and governance review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, commitment scorecard, retention-risk scorecard, loyalty scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
