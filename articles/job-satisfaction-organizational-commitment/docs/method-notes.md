# Method Notes

This scaffold demonstrates how job satisfaction, organizational commitment, retention risk, and symbolic commitment risk can be modeled while preserving interpretive caution.

## Conceptual model

The model treats durable employee attachment as an interaction among:

- leadership support
- reward fairness
- developmental opportunity
- workload balance
- institutional trust
- cultural alignment
- affective commitment
- continuance commitment
- normative commitment
- role insecurity
- exhaustion pressure

## Suggested methods

- descriptive diagnostics
- unit-level work-attitude dashboards
- logistic regression for retention risk
- symbolic commitment-risk review
- satisfaction and commitment scenario analysis
- sensitivity analysis
- qualitative validation
- stay-interview and exit-interview review
- compensation and promotion fairness review
- workload sustainability review
- culture and institutional trust review

## Methodological caution

Job satisfaction and organizational commitment are partly measurable but not fully captured by metrics. They involve meaning, power, mobility, fairness, trust, workload, leadership behavior, development opportunity, economic constraint, benefit dependence, and lived experience. Quantitative outputs should be paired with qualitative research and ethical governance.
