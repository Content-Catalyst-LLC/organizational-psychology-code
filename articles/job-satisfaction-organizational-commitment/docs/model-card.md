# Model Card: Synthetic Job Satisfaction, Organizational Commitment, and Retention-Risk Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Work-Attitude, Commitment, and Retention-System Risk Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, work-attitude review, retention-system education, fairness and workload analysis, leadership and culture review, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- commitment scoring of workers
- retention-risk scoring of individual workers
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real units, real workplaces, real leaders, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- job satisfaction scores
- organizational commitment scores
- retention-system risk scores
- symbolic commitment-risk summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as unit and institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker and stakeholder consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex satisfaction and commitment phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure power, history, benefit dependence, constrained mobility, hidden labor, unfairness, retaliation risk, workload, and lived experience.
- Satisfaction and commitment cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
