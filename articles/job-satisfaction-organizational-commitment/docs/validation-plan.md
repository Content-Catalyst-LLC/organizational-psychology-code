# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, surveillance, commitment-scoring, retention-risk scoring, performance-ranking, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent work-system and institutional conditions rather than individual worker traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe a work condition, leadership condition, fairness system, culture, workload, or institutional trust condition?
- Does it describe satisfaction, commitment, healthy attachment, constrained retention, or system-level retention risk?
- Does it describe the environment rather than an individual's loyalty, positivity, productivity, or psychological status?
- Could it be misused to label or rank individuals?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- fear of retaliation
- survey nonresponse and self-censorship
- aggregate scores hiding marginalized experience
- hidden labor and uneven burden
- workload intensity and staffing
- advancement, compensation, and development patterns
- leadership and supervision differences
- benefit dependence or constrained mobility
- context behind numeric indicators

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match work-attitude review evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, loyalty scoring, commitment scoring, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

A work-attitude review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- stronger leadership support
- fairer rewards and recognition
- better development pathways
- improved workload balance
- stronger institutional trust
- greater cultural coherence
- clearer retention-system signals
- reduced constrained retention
