# Leadership in Organizational Psychology

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Leadership in Organizational Psychology.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying leadership as a multilevel organizational process shaped by trust generation, communication clarity, motivational support, legitimacy quality, adaptive coordination, psychological safety, role clarity, ethical accountability, decision transparency, voice access, ambiguity pressure, distrust pressure, fragmentation pressure, overload pressure, voice-suppression risk, legitimacy decay, and institutional stability.

The scaffold treats leadership as a system-level and institutional construct. It is not a leader popularity score, charisma score, employee attitude score, loyalty score, political-influence score, dissent-tracking system, or psychological assessment tool. It supports institutional learning about how influence becomes credible, accountable, ethical, and capable of supporting coordinated action under uncertainty.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- leadership-system diagnostics
- trust, legitimacy, and communication review
- psychological safety and voice analysis
- adaptive coordination and institutional stability simulation
- fragmentation, ambiguity, distrust, and overload review
- ethical leadership and accountability education
- leadership development and institutional-learning workflows
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee screening, employee ranking, worker motivation scoring, leadership-loyalty scoring, political-influence scoring, dissent tracking, psychological assessment, workplace surveillance, employment decision-making, or worker risk labeling.

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, leadership-system review, trust/communication/legitimacy/psychological-safety diagnostics, organizational learning, leadership-development education, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, productivity-ranking tools, leadership-loyalty scoring tools, political-influence scoring tools, dissent-tracking tools, psychological assessment tools, or automated personnel decision-making systems.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the leadership system, work system, team environment, communication routine, decision process, psychological-safety climate, organizational unit, leadership-development system, accountability structure, or institutional context—not the worth, productivity, loyalty, motivation, morality, psychological status, employability, or legitimacy of any individual worker.
