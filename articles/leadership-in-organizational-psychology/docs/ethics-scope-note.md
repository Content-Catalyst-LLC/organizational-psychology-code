# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, leadership-system review, trust/communication/legitimacy/psychological-safety diagnostics, organizational learning, leadership-development education, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for employee screening, employment selection, hiring, promotion, compensation, discipline, termination, workplace surveillance, individual performance management, productivity ranking, leadership-loyalty scoring, worker-risk scoring, political-influence scoring, dissent tracking, psychological assessment, or automated personnel decision-making.

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the leadership system, work system, team environment, communication routine, decision process, psychological-safety climate, organizational unit, leadership-development system, accountability structure, or institutional context.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as motivated, unmotivated, loyal, disloyal, productive, unproductive, dissenting, compliant, noncompliant, high-risk, low-risk, resistant, suitable, unsuitable, psychologically deficient, politically influential, politically risky, or employable.
