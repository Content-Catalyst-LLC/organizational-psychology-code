# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and leadership-system review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

- Has leadership earned belief through consistency and follow-through?
- Are decisions experienced as fair, competent, and procedurally legitimate?
- Do leaders explain priorities, constraints, tradeoffs, and uncertainty clearly?
- Can people raise concerns, dissent, errors, or weak signals without retaliation?
- Are lower-power voices protected?
- Are responsibilities, goals, decision rights, and escalation paths clear?
- Are teams aligned across functions and expertise boundaries?
- Can leadership behavior be reviewed and corrected?
- Are authority and power used with restraint?
- Are leaders asking people to sustain effort under unreasonable strain?
- Is purpose language being used to normalize overwork?

Responsible-use reminder: this checklist supports institutional learning and leadership-development review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, political-influence scorecard, dissent-tracking system, loyalty scorecard, productivity scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
