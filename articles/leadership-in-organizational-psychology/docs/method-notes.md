# Method Notes

This scaffold demonstrates how leadership capacity, institutional stability, performance risk, voice-suppression risk, legitimacy decay, and fragmentation risk can be modeled while preserving interpretive caution.

The model treats leadership capacity as an interaction among trust generation, communication clarity, motivational support, legitimacy quality, adaptive coordination, psychological safety, role clarity, ethical accountability, decision transparency, voice access, ambiguity pressure, distrust pressure, fragmentation pressure, and overload pressure.

Suggested methods include descriptive diagnostics, unit-level leadership dashboards, logistic regression for performance risk, legitimacy decay review, voice-suppression risk review, institutional stability scenario analysis, sensitivity analysis, qualitative validation, leadership feedback review, communication audit, decision-process review, meeting observation, psychological safety assessment, workload review, and role-clarity review.

Leadership is partly measurable but not fully captured by metrics. Quantitative outputs should be paired with qualitative research and ethical governance.
