# Model Card: Synthetic Leadership Capacity and Institutional Stability Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Leadership Capacity, Trust, Communication, Legitimacy, Psychological Safety, and Institutional Stability Workflow

## Intended use

Synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, leadership-system review, trust/communication/legitimacy/psychological-safety diagnostics, organizational learning, leadership-development education, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

Employee screening, employee ranking, productivity ranking, leadership-loyalty scoring, political-influence scoring, dissent tracking, employment selection, hiring, promotion, compensation, discipline, termination, workplace surveillance, individual performance management, or psychological assessment.

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real leaders, real units, real teams, real workplaces, real leadership programs, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce leadership capacity scores, institutional stability scores, leadership-system risk scores, performance risk summaries, voice-suppression risk summaries, legitimacy-decay risk summaries, fragmentation risk summaries, review priority categories, and scenario comparisons.
