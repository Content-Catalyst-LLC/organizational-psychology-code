# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, surveillance, dissent-tracking, productivity-ranking, leadership-loyalty scoring, political-influence scoring, or psychological assessment tool.

## Construct validation

Review whether variables represent leadership-system and institutional conditions rather than individual worker traits. The measure should describe leadership behavior, decision process, communication quality, trust, role clarity, legitimacy, psychological safety, voice access, or institutional context—not an individual worker's loyalty, motivation, positivity, dissent, productivity, or psychological status.

## Data quality review

In real-world research, examine source provenance, fear of retaliation, self-censorship and silence, power distance, recency effects, reputational bias, unequal experiences across roles and identities, decision-process documentation, meeting behavior, voice patterns, workload, and role clarity.

## Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, dissent tracking, leadership-loyalty scoring, political-influence scoring, productivity ranking, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## Action validation

A leadership-system review is only useful if it leads to clearer communication, stronger trust, stronger legitimacy, better role clarity, safer voice, stronger ethical accountability, reduced ambiguity, reduced fragmentation, and reduced overload pressure.
