# Leadership Styles and Organizational Performance

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Leadership Styles and Organizational Performance.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying leadership style as a system-level condition shaped by participation, structure, communication quality, trust generation, developmental support, psychological safety, decision fit, cultural alignment, control pressure, ambiguity risk, disengagement pressure, voice suppression, and performance risk.

The scaffold treats leadership style not as personality branding, leader popularity, individual employee positivity, worker loyalty, or political influence. It treats leadership behavior as an institutional condition that shapes motivation, trust, voice, decision quality, learning, coordination, accountability, culture, and performance.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- leadership-system diagnostics
- leadership style-fit and context analysis
- communication, trust, and psychological safety review
- control-pressure and ambiguity-risk analysis
- developmental-support and coaching review
- participatory and transformational leadership education
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee screening, employee ranking, individual worker scoring, dissent tracking, leadership-loyalty scoring, psychological assessment, workplace surveillance, employment decision-making, or worker risk labeling.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models leadership effectiveness and leadership-system risk as system-level constructs shaped by:

- participative behavior
- structural guidance
- communication quality
- trust generation
- developmental support
- psychological safety
- decision-style fit
- cultural alignment
- control pressure
- ambiguity risk
- disengagement pressure
- voice suppression risk
- institutional performance risk
- leadership style misfit risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, leadership-system review, communication/trust/style-fit analysis, psychological-safety and work-design education, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, productivity-ranking tools, leadership-loyalty scoring tools, dissent-tracking tools, psychological assessment tools, or automated personnel decision-making systems.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the leadership system, work system, team environment, communication routine, decision process, psychological-safety climate, organizational unit, leadership-development system, or institutional context—not the worth, productivity, loyalty, motivation, morality, psychological status, employability, or legitimacy of any individual worker.

## Suggested workflow

1. Read `docs/ethics-scope-note.md`.
2. Review `docs/research-design.md`.
3. Inspect `docs/data-dictionary.md`.
