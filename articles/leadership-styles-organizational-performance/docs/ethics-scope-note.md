# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, leadership-system review, communication/trust/style-fit analysis, psychological-safety and work-design education, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- leadership-loyalty scoring
- worker-risk scoring
- dissent tracking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the leadership system, work system, team environment, communication routine, decision process, psychological-safety climate, organizational unit, leadership-development system, or institutional context.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as motivated, unmotivated, loyal, disloyal, productive, unproductive, dissenting, compliant, noncompliant, high-risk, low-risk, suitable, unsuitable, psychologically deficient, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Leadership effectiveness and performance-risk signals may reflect unclear priorities, weak communication, excessive control pressure, leader absence, style-context mismatch, low psychological safety, low trust, weak developmental support, or institutional constraints.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Leadership-style analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear naming weak leadership, distrust, control pressure, role ambiguity, voice suppression, or lived work experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible leadership review.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
