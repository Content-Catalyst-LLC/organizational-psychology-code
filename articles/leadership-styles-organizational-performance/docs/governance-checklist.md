# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and leadership-system review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Participation and voice

- Do leaders invite meaningful input before decisions where expertise is distributed?
- Is participation real, or merely symbolic?
- Can lower-power employees raise concerns safely?

## Structure and clarity

- Are roles, goals, decision rights, and escalation pathways clear?
- Does the leader provide enough guidance without unnecessary control?
- Are employees left with autonomy or abandonment?

## Communication

- Are priorities and rationales explained clearly?
- Does communication reduce ambiguity, or create rumor and confusion?
- Are uncertainty and tradeoffs acknowledged honestly?

## Trust and fairness

- Are leaders consistent, credible, and procedurally fair?
- Do employees trust that bad news can be raised without retaliation?
- Are standards applied consistently across status levels?

## Style fit

- Does leadership style match task demands, urgency, expertise distribution, risk, and team maturity?
- Is directive leadership being used only where genuinely warranted?
- Is participatory leadership structured enough to maintain accountability?

## Development and psychological safety

- Do leaders build capability through feedback, coaching, and learning?
- Are mistakes used for learning rather than reputation threat?
- Is dissent treated as useful information or disloyalty?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and leadership-development review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, dissent-tracking system, loyalty scorecard, productivity scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
