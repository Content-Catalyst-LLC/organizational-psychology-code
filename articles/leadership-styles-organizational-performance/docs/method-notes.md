# Method Notes

This scaffold demonstrates how leadership effectiveness, institutional performance risk, leadership style misfit, and voice-suppression risk can be modeled while preserving interpretive caution.

## Conceptual model

The model treats leadership effectiveness as an interaction among:

- participative behavior
- structural guidance
- communication quality
- trust generation
- developmental support
- psychological safety
- decision-style fit
- cultural alignment
- control pressure
- ambiguity risk
- disengagement pressure

## Suggested methods

- descriptive diagnostics
- unit-level leadership-system dashboards
- logistic regression for performance risk
- style-fit review
- voice-suppression risk review
- scenario analysis
- sensitivity analysis
- qualitative validation
- meeting and decision-process observation
- communication audit
- leadership feedback review
- psychological safety assessment
- work-system and role-clarity review

## Methodological caution

Leadership style is partly measurable but not fully captured by metrics. It involves behavior, interpretation, power, trust, culture, voice, expertise, accountability, task context, workload, identity, and lived experience. Quantitative outputs should be paired with qualitative research and ethical governance.
