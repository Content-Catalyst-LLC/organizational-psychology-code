# Model Card: Synthetic Leadership Style Effectiveness and Institutional Performance Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Leadership Style, Trust, Communication, Psychological Safety, and Institutional Performance Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, leadership-system review, communication/trust/style-fit analysis, psychological-safety and work-design education, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- productivity ranking
- leadership-loyalty scoring
- dissent tracking
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real leaders, real units, real teams, real workplaces, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- leadership effectiveness scores
- leadership-system risk scores
- institutional performance risk summaries
- voice-suppression risk summaries
- style-misfit risk summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as unit and institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker and stakeholder consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex leadership, culture, power, and performance phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure history, retaliation risk, hidden labor, role hierarchy, status differences, resource constraints, and lived experience.
- Leadership style cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
