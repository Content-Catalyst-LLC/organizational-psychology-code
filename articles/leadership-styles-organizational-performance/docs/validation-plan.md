# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, surveillance, dissent-tracking, productivity-ranking, leadership-loyalty scoring, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent leadership-system and institutional conditions rather than individual worker traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe leadership behavior, decision process, communication quality, trust, role clarity, psychological safety, style fit, or institutional context?
- Does it describe the leadership environment rather than an individual worker's loyalty, motivation, positivity, dissent, productivity, or psychological status?
- Could it be misused to identify critics, punish employee feedback, rank workers, or surveil team sentiment?
- Does the measure support institutional learning rather than individual blame?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- fear of retaliation
- self-censorship and silence
- power distance between leaders and employees
- recency effects and reputational bias
- unequal experiences across roles and identities
- decision-process documentation
- meeting behavior and voice patterns
- context behind numeric indicators

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match leadership-review evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, dissent tracking, loyalty scoring, productivity ranking, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

A leadership-system review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- clearer communication
- stronger trust
- better structural guidance
- more meaningful participation
- safer voice channels
- stronger psychological safety
- reduced control pressure
- reduced ambiguity
- better leadership style fit
- improved developmental support
