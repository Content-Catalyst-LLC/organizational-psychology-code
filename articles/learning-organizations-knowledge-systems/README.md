# Learning Organizations and Knowledge Systems

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Learning Organizations and Knowledge Systems.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying organizational learning as a system-level institutional capability.

The scaffold treats learning organizations as institutions that convert experience into durable collective intelligence. It focuses on the conditions under which information becomes interpretation, interpretation becomes memory, memory becomes shared practice, and shared practice becomes adaptive institutional change.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- learning-organization concept exploration
- institutional knowledge-governance design
- reproducible teaching workflows
- systems-oriented research scaffolding
- responsible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee scoring, workplace surveillance, psychological assessment, or employment decision-making.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models organizational learning capacity as a system-level construct shaped by:

- information quality
- interpretive openness
- institutional memory retention
- cross-boundary communication flow
- psychological safety
- governance support
- environmental and task complexity
- silo intensity
- incentive distortion
- turnover pressure
- adaptive improvement
- knowledge decay risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, or psychological assessment tools.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the institution, work system, team process, knowledge system, governance condition, or organizational learning environment—not the worth, intelligence, learning capacity, adaptability, or employability of an individual worker.

## Suggested workflow

1. Read `docs/ethics-scope-note.md`.
2. Inspect `docs/research-design.md`.
3. Review `docs/data-dictionary.md`.
