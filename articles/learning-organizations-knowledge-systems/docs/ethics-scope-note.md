# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, organizational knowledge governance, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, work system, team process, knowledge system, governance structure, or institutional learning condition.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as adaptable, unadaptable, high-learning, low-learning, psychologically safe, unsafe, suitable, unsuitable, resilient, fragile, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Weak learning signals may reflect structural underinvestment, unclear authority, fear of retaliation, poor documentation systems, excessive workload, turnover, siloed platforms, distorted incentives, or leadership defensiveness.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Learning systems should increase institutional responsibility, not worker exposure. If a workflow causes employees to fear reporting mistakes, raising concerns, or sharing knowledge, it is undermining organizational learning.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
