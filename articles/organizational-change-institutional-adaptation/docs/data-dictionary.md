# Data Dictionary

## `data/raw/adaptive_change_capacity_synthetic.csv`

This synthetic dataset describes organizational units across repeated periods during institutional change. It is designed for methods demonstration only.

| Field | Description | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| `unit_id` | Synthetic organizational unit identifier | Categorical |
| `period` | Synthetic observation period | Integer |
| `sensing_quality` | Ability to detect environmental, internal, stakeholder, and operational signals | 0–100 |
| `interpretive_coherence` | Degree of shared sensemaking across leadership and organizational units | 0–100 |
| `governance_alignment` | Alignment of decision rights, accountability, sequencing, and oversight | 0–100 |
| `learning_capability` | Ability to incorporate feedback, revise assumptions, and update practice | 0–100 |
| `resource_flexibility` | Ability to redirect staffing, budget, tools, time, and capabilities | 0–100 |
| `institutional_legitimacy` | Credibility, fairness, and justified purpose of the change process | 0–100 |
| `organizational_inertia` | Degree of rigidity in routines, structures, assumptions, and dependencies | 0–100 |
| `fragmentation` | Degree of siloing, inconsistent interpretation, or disconnected implementation | 0–100 |
| `resistance_friction` | Implementation friction arising from threat, mistrust, overload, or disagreement | 0–100 |
| `environmental_pressure` | External or internal pressure requiring adaptation | 0–100 |
| `successful_transformation` | Synthetic binary indicator of meaningful adaptive change | 0/1 |
| `symbolic_change` | Synthetic binary indicator of formal activity without deeper transformation | 0/1 |

## Interpretation

Variables are institutional, team-process, and change-system indicators. They should not be interpreted as measures of individual adaptability, attitude, loyalty, resilience, performance, or psychological status.
