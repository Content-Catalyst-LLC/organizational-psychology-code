# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, culture review, organizational development education, legitimacy analysis, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- cultural-fit scoring
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, cultural system, governance structure, institutional field, stakeholder relationship, organizational unit, subculture, work system, or legitimacy condition.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as aligned, misaligned, loyal, disloyal, culturally fit, culturally unfit, resistant, nonresistant, high-risk, suitable, unsuitable, resilient, fragile, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Culture-risk signals may reflect structural contradictions, unclear authority, low psychological safety, weak leadership credibility, misaligned incentives, selective accountability, stakeholder mistrust, external pressure, or subcultural fragmentation.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Culture analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear speaking honestly about culture, contradiction, leadership behavior, norms, safety, or lived institutional experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible cultural stewardship.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
