# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and cultural review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Value-practice alignment

- Do official values match resource allocation, promotion, incentives, and crisis behavior?
- Are values credible when they become costly?
- Are contradictions documented and repaired?

## Normative consistency

- Are behavioral expectations understandable across units?
- Do norms support voice, learning, cooperation, and accountability?
- Are norms applied fairly across hierarchy levels?

## Leadership credibility

- Do leaders model the culture they claim to want?
- Are high-status actors held accountable for cultural violations?
- Do leaders respond to bad news with learning or punishment?

## Psychological safety

- Can members raise concerns, dissent, and report errors without disproportionate penalty?
- Is silence interpreted cautiously rather than as trust?
- Are frontline and low-power voices treated as sources of institutional intelligence?

## Subcultural variation

- Where do professional, regional, functional, and hierarchy-level subcultures differ?
- Which differences preserve expertise and local knowledge?
- Which differences create fragmentation or inequity?

## Incentive distortion

- Do metrics and rewards teach a different culture than official values?
- Are people rewarded for hoarding information, hiding failure, or overworking?
- Are risk, safety, and learning properly incentivized?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and governance review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, cultural-fit screen, loyalty scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
