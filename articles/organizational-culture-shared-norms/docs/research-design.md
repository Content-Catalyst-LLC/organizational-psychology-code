# Research Design

This scaffold supports research-methods demonstration for studying organizational culture and shared norms as system-level institutional conditions rather than morale, branding, employee sentiment, or individual cultural fit.

## Research question

How can cultural coherence and shared-norm integrity be represented, simulated, and reviewed as system-level constructs shaped by value alignment, normative consistency, leadership credibility, psychological safety, shared meaning, contradiction, fragmentation, incentive distortion, and external pressure?

## Conceptual assumptions

1. Organizational culture is a patterned system of meaning, norms, assumptions, and reinforcement.
2. Culture becomes visible through gaps between espoused values and lived practice.
3. Psychological safety is central to learning, voice, and institutional adaptation.
4. Subcultural variation is normal in complex institutions and should not be reduced automatically to dysfunction.
5. Culture analytics must support institutional learning rather than worker loyalty scoring, cultural-fit screening, surveillance, or employment decisions.

## Synthetic modeling aims

The scaffold demonstrates how to:

- generate a cultural coherence score
- estimate strong institutional performance outcomes
- identify symbolic culture risk
- summarize unit-level cultural conditions
- model performance integrity under contradiction and fragmentation
- document responsible-use boundaries

## Appropriate interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as prompts for institutional inquiry. A high-risk synthetic profile should not be read as a real-world diagnosis. In actual organizational research, quantitative signals require qualitative validation through cultural review, interviews, observation, governance review, incident analysis, ethics review, promotion-pattern analysis, and participatory interpretation with affected groups.
