# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, loyalty-scoring, productivity-ranking, cultural-fit, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent cultural-system and institutional conditions rather than individual worker traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe cultural norms and institutional conditions?
- Does it describe value alignment, psychological safety, shared meaning, contradiction, or incentive distortion?
- Does it describe organizational conditions rather than personal attitude or cultural fit?
- Could it be misused to label or rank individuals?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- employee fear of retaliation
- underreporting of culture problems
- leadership self-report bias
- subcultural differences hidden by aggregate data
- stakeholder exclusion
- cultural narratives that conceal power or material constraints
- context behind numeric indicators

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match cultural-review evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, loyalty scoring, cultural-fit screening, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

A cultural review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- stronger psychological safety
- lower contradiction
- better value-practice alignment
- reduced incentive distortion
- more honest subcultural translation
- better stakeholder recognition
- stronger learning systems
- credible accountability
