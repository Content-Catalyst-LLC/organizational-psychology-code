# Organizational Identity and Institutional Legitimacy

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Organizational Identity and Institutional Legitimacy.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying organizational identity as an institutional, symbolic, relational, and governance condition.

The scaffold treats organizational identity not as branding, image management, or individual loyalty, but as a system-level construct shaped by mission clarity, value-practice alignment, narrative continuity, leadership credibility, behavioral consistency, internal fragmentation, identity contradiction, external legitimacy pressure, stakeholder trust, and adaptive identity capacity.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- organizational identity analysis
- institutional legitimacy review
- stakeholder trust and recognition modeling
- governance and identity-stewardship diagnostics
- institutional learning and accountability review
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee scoring, cultural-loyalty scoring, workplace surveillance, psychological assessment, productivity ranking, employment decision-making, or individual alignment labeling.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models identity coherence and legitimacy as system-level constructs shaped by:

- mission clarity
- value-practice alignment
- narrative continuity
- leadership credibility
- behavioral consistency
- internal fragmentation
- identity contradiction
- legitimacy pressure
- stakeholder trust
- adaptive identity capacity
- symbolic identity risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, organizational identity review, legitimacy analysis, governance review, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, cultural-loyalty scoring tools, cultural-fit tools, productivity-ranking tools, psychological assessment tools, or legitimacy scoring tools for workers or leaders.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, identity system, governance structure, stakeholder relationship, institutional field, organizational unit, cultural system, or legitimacy condition—not the worth, loyalty, identity, attitude, cultural fit, employability, psychological status, morality, or legitimacy of an individual worker.

1. Read `docs/ethics-scope-note.md`.
