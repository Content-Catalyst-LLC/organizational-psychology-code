# Data Dictionary

## `data/raw/identity_legitimacy_synthetic.csv`

This synthetic dataset describes organizational units across repeated identity-legitimacy review periods. It is designed for methods demonstration only.

| Field | Description | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| `unit_id` | Synthetic organizational unit identifier | Categorical |
| `period` | Synthetic observation period | Integer |
| `mission_clarity` | Clarity of institutional purpose, role, and identity commitments | 0–100 |
| `value_practice_alignment` | Degree to which declared values align with lived institutional practice | 0–100 |
| `narrative_continuity` | Coherence of identity narrative across history, change, and future direction | 0–100 |
| `leadership_credibility` | Credibility of leadership identity work based on behavior, governance, and consistency | 0–100 |
| `behavioral_consistency` | Consistency of identity-relevant behavior across units, decisions, and public claims | 0–100 |
| `internal_fragmentation` | Degree of identity fragmentation across units, professions, regions, or stakeholder groups | 0–100 |
| `identity_contradiction` | Degree of visible contradiction between identity claims and institutional conduct | 0–100 |
| `legitimacy_pressure` | External pressure from stakeholders, fields, regulators, communities, or public expectations | 0–100 |
| `stakeholder_legitimacy` | Synthetic binary indicator of stakeholder legitimacy recognition | 0/1 |
| `symbolic_identity_risk` | Synthetic binary indicator of identity presentation without deeper institutional coherence | 0/1 |

## Interpretation

Variables are institutional, unit-level, identity-system, and legitimacy-system indicators. They should not be interpreted as measures of individual loyalty, cultural fit, identity alignment, performance, personality, legitimacy, morality, or psychological status.
