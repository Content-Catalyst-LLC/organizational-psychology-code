# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, organizational identity review, legitimacy analysis, governance review, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- cultural-fit scoring
- worker legitimacy scoring
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, identity system, governance structure, stakeholder relationship, institutional field, organizational unit, cultural system, or legitimacy condition.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as aligned, misaligned, loyal, disloyal, legitimate, illegitimate, culturally fit, culturally unfit, resistant, nonresistant, high-risk, suitable, unsuitable, resilient, fragile, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Identity-legitimacy risk signals may reflect structural contradictions, value-practice gaps, public accountability failures, leadership credibility problems, historical erasure, stakeholder mistrust, mission drift, field-level pressure, or subcultural fragmentation.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Identity analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear speaking honestly about mission, values, contradiction, public trust, leadership behavior, or lived institutional experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible identity stewardship.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
