# Method Notes

This scaffold demonstrates how identity coherence and legitimacy can be modeled while preserving interpretive caution.

## Conceptual model

The model treats identity coherence as an interaction among:

- mission clarity
- value-practice alignment
- narrative continuity
- leadership credibility
- behavioral consistency
- internal fragmentation
- identity contradiction
- legitimacy pressure

## Suggested methods

- descriptive diagnostics
- unit-level identity-legitimacy review dashboards
- logistic regression for stakeholder legitimacy
- symbolic identity-risk review
- identity risk scoring
- scenario analysis
- sensitivity analysis
- qualitative validation
- governance review
- stakeholder trust review

## Methodological caution

Organizational identity and legitimacy are partly measurable but not fully captured by metrics. They involve meaning, memory, power, recognition, history, values, stakeholder trust, field-level expectations, and lived institutional experience. Quantitative outputs should be paired with qualitative research and ethical governance.
