# Model Card: Synthetic Organizational Identity and Institutional Legitimacy Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Identity Coherence, Legitimacy, and Symbolic Identity Risk Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, organizational identity review, legitimacy analysis, governance review, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- cultural-fit scoring
- worker legitimacy scoring
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real executives, real teams, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- identity coherence scores
- identity risk scores
- stakeholder legitimacy predictions
- symbolic identity-risk summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker and stakeholder consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex identity and legitimacy phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure power, history, memory, field conditions, and lived experience.
- Organizational identity cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
