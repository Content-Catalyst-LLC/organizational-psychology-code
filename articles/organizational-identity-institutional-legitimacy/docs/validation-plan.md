# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, loyalty-scoring, productivity-ranking, legitimacy-scoring, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent identity-system and legitimacy-system conditions rather than individual worker traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe institutional identity coherence?
- Does it describe value-practice alignment, narrative continuity, stakeholder legitimacy, or identity contradiction?
- Does it describe organizational conditions rather than personal loyalty or cultural fit?
- Could it be misused to label or rank individuals?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- stakeholder representation
- missing voices
- fear of retaliation
- public-relations bias
- leadership self-report bias
- historical erasure
- aggregate measures hiding subcultural conflict
- context behind numeric indicators

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match identity-review evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, loyalty scoring, legitimacy scoring, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

An identity-legitimacy review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- better value-practice alignment
- more honest narrative continuity
- reduced identity contradiction
- stronger stakeholder recognition
- better participation in identity work
- more credible leadership conduct
- clearer legitimacy repair
- stronger identity stewardship
