# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, work system, department, team process, governance structure, or institutional condition.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as resilient, nonresilient, high-risk, fragile, adaptable, or unsuitable.

## Responsible interpretation

Organizational resilience should be interpreted as a system-level capacity. Weak resilience signals may reflect structural underinvestment, unclear authority, poor staffing design, harmful incentives, weak communication systems, or unsafe reporting climates.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
