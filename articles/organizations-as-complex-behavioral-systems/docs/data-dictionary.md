# Data Dictionary

## `data/raw/complex_behavioral_systems_synthetic.csv`

This synthetic dataset describes people nested in organizational units. It is designed for methods demonstration only.

| Field | Description | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| `unit_id` | Synthetic organizational unit identifier | Categorical |
| `person_id` | Synthetic person identifier within a unit | Categorical |
| `capability` | Task-relevant competence or capacity under current work conditions | 0–100 |
| `motivation` | Energy, engagement, and willingness to invest effort | 0–100 |
| `role_clarity` | Clarity of responsibilities, goals, decision rights, and expectations | 0–100 |
| `psychological_safety` | Ability to ask questions, raise concerns, admit uncertainty, and speak honestly | 0–100 |
| `leadership_trust` | Credibility, consistency, and trustworthiness of leadership | 0–100 |
| `cultural_coherence` | Degree to which stated values, norms, incentives, and behavior align | 0–100 |
| `incentive_alignment` | Degree to which incentives support desired, ethical, learning-oriented behavior | 0–100 |
| `information_flow` | Quality, timeliness, completeness, and trustworthiness of system information | 0–100 |
| `learning_capacity` | Capacity to notice feedback, examine errors, revise assumptions, and improve routines | 0–100 |
| `redundancy_slack` | Degree of reserve capacity, backup knowledge, staffing flexibility, and recovery space | 0–100 |
| `workload_pressure` | Degree of demand, time pressure, strain, and work intensity | 0–100 |
| `silence_risk` | Risk that people withhold concerns, weak signals, error information, or dissent | 0–100 |
| `fragmentation_pressure` | Degree of siloing, misalignment, information separation, and coordination breakdown | 0–100 |
| `environmental_uncertainty` | Degree of external instability, ambiguity, disruption, or shifting demand | 0–100 |
| `behavior_quality` | Synthetic outcome representing constructive person-system behavior | 0–100 |
| `complex_system_risk` | Synthetic system-level risk score | 0–100 |

## Interpretation

Variables are demonstration constructs for organizational psychology education. They are not real assessments and must not be used to judge individual employees, screen candidates, rank workers, track dissent, or automate employment decisions.
