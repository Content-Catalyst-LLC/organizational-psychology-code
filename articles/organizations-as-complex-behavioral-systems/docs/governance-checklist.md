# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and complex behavioral system review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## System diagnosis

- Are problems being analyzed at the right level: individual, team, unit, organization, or environment?
- Are role clarity, workload, incentives, feedback loops, and resource conditions included?
- Are leaders asking what the system rewards before blaming individual behavior?

## Feedback and learning

- Can people surface bad news, weak signals, errors, and uncertainty safely?
- Are feedback loops acted upon or merely collected?
- Does the organization learn from problems or assign blame?

## Trust and voice

- Is psychological safety strong enough to support candor?
- Are lower-status voices protected?
- Is silence being mistaken for agreement?
- Are anti-retaliation safeguards real and trusted?

## Resilience and fragility

- Does the system have redundancy, slack, and recovery capacity?
- Are informal brokers carrying hidden coordination labor?
- Are overload, fragmentation, and silence interacting?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and responsible organizational development. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, loyalty scorecard, dissent-tracking system, productivity scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
