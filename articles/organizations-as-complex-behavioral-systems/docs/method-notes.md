# Method Notes

This scaffold demonstrates a systems-oriented approach to organizations as complex behavioral systems.

## Conceptual model

Behavior quality is modeled as a function of capability, motivation, role clarity, psychological safety, leadership trust, cultural coherence, incentive alignment, information flow, learning capacity, redundancy/slack, workload pressure, silence risk, fragmentation pressure, and environmental uncertainty.

Complex system risk is modeled as an interaction among weak safety, low trust, weak cultural coherence, poor incentive alignment, weak information flow, low learning capacity, low redundancy, high workload, silence risk, fragmentation, and uncertainty.

## Suggested methods

- descriptive unit summaries
- multilevel modeling
- system-risk composite indices
- scenario analysis
- feedback-loop interpretation
- sensitivity analysis
- qualitative validation
- network and information-flow review
- psychological safety and voice review
- workload and resilience review

## Methodological caution

Synthetic data cannot validate real-world conclusions. Real organizational psychology research requires careful design, ethical review, validated measures, attention to power and privacy, anti-retaliation protections, and interpretive humility. Complex system models should prompt inquiry, not automated judgment.
