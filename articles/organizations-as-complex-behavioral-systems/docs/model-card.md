# Model Card: Synthetic Complex Behavioral Systems Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Organizations as Complex Behavioral Systems Model

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, organizational psychology education, complex behavioral systems analysis, and reproducible examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- productivity ranking
- loyalty scoring
- dissent tracking
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real people, real teams, real organizations, or real institutions.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- behavior quality summaries
- complex system risk summaries
- unit-level review priorities
- model coefficients
- scenario comparisons
- system-risk interpretation notes

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as work-system inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world adaptation would require domain expertise, validated measurement, qualitative validation, privacy review, anti-retaliation safeguards, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot support real-world causal claims.
- Human work behavior and system behavior are simplified into demonstration variables.
- Quantitative summaries can obscure lived experience, power, history, inequality, and institutional context.
- Network and feedback-loop interpretations are sensitive and must not be used to identify dissenters or critics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
