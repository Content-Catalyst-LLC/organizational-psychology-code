# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration only.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether each variable represents complex system and work-system conditions rather than personal worth, loyalty, employability, or psychological status.

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- measurement validity
- source provenance
- sample representativeness
- fear of retaliation
- confidentiality
- missing data
- unequal experiences across roles and identities
- context behind numeric indicators
- whether silence reflects agreement, fear, overload, learned futility, or low efficacy

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- sensitivity to weighting choices
- coefficient stability
- uncertainty
- subgroup interpretation
- qualitative plausibility
- whether outputs match observed organizational conditions
- whether feedback-loop interpretations are grounded in process evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that outputs cannot reasonably be repurposed for employee screening, surveillance, productivity ranking, discipline, dissent tracking, loyalty scoring, or psychological assessment.

## 5. Action validation

Track whether findings support institutional improvements such as clearer roles, better communication, stronger leadership trust, safer voice, workload repair, lower silence risk, better information flow, more redundancy, and stronger learning capacity.
