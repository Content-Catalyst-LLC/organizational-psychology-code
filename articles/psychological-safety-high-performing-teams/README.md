# Psychological Safety in High-Performing Teams

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Psychological Safety in High-Performing Teams.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying psychological safety as a team-level and institutional condition that shapes voice, error reporting, learning behavior, dissent, status dynamics, leadership openness, and adaptive performance.

The scaffold treats psychological safety not as comfort, niceness, agreement, low standards, employee personality, or individual courage. It treats psychological safety as a shared team condition shaped by leadership openness, inclusion quality, learning review quality, knowledge sharing, blame intensity, status asymmetry, perceived penalty, workload pressure, voice behavior, reporting climate, and team performance integrity.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- psychological safety analysis
- voice and error-reporting diagnostics
- team-learning review
- leadership openness and inclusion analysis
- blame-culture and status-asymmetry review
- meeting-design and participation analysis
- institutional learning and accountability review
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee screening, cultural-loyalty scoring, courage scoring, psychological assessment, productivity ranking, workplace surveillance, employment decision-making, or individual safety labeling.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models psychological safety and strong team performance as system-level constructs shaped by:

- leadership openness
- inclusion quality
- learning review quality
- knowledge sharing
- blame intensity
- status asymmetry
- perceived penalty for speaking
- workload pressure
- voice behavior
- team learning capacity
- strong team performance
- symbolic voice risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, team-learning review, psychological safety education, leadership and voice-system analysis, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, cultural-loyalty scoring tools, courage-scoring tools, worker voice-scoring tools, productivity-ranking tools, psychological assessment tools, or automated personnel decision-making systems.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the team environment, organizational unit, meeting system, reporting climate, leadership routine, learning system, governance structure, institutional field, stakeholder relationship, or team-level safety condition—not the worth, courage, loyalty, personality, employability, psychological status, morality, or legitimacy of any individual worker.
