# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, team-learning review, psychological safety education, leadership and voice-system analysis, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- courage scoring
- voice scoring of workers
- worker-risk scoring
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the team environment, organizational unit, meeting system, reporting climate, leadership routine, learning system, governance structure, institutional field, stakeholder relationship, or team-level safety condition.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as safe, unsafe, brave, fearful, aligned, misaligned, loyal, disloyal, resistant, nonresistant, high-risk, suitable, unsuitable, resilient, fragile, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Psychological-safety risk signals may reflect structural contradictions, unclear authority, high blame intensity, low leadership openness, weak inclusion quality, status asymmetry, retaliation memory, workload pressure, poor meeting design, or weak learning systems.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Psychological safety analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear speaking honestly about safety, error, dissent, uncertainty, leadership behavior, or lived team experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible psychological safety review.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
