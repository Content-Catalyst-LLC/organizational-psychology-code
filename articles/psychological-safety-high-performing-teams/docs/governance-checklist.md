# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and psychological safety review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Leadership openness

- Do leaders invite input before decisions harden?
- Do leaders respond constructively to bad news?
- Do leaders protect people who raise inconvenient concerns?

## Inclusion quality

- Who speaks in consequential meetings?
- Who is interrupted, ignored, or asked to over-prove?
- Do lower-status members have meaningful participation?

## Error reporting

- Are mistakes and near misses reported early?
- Does reporting trigger learning or blame?
- Are low incident counts interpreted cautiously?

## Learning review

- Do retrospectives and postmortems produce system change?
- Are lessons documented and revisited?
- Are error analysis and accountability separated carefully?

## Status asymmetry

- Can junior, marginalized, or non-dominant members challenge assumptions?
- Does professional status determine whose knowledge counts?
- Are dissenting specialists heard before failure?

## Perceived penalty

- What happens after someone speaks up?
- Are speakers protected from subtle retaliation?
- Does voice change outcomes or only produce documentation?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and governance review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, courage scorecard, voice scorecard, loyalty scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
