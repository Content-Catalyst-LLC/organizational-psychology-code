# Model Card: Synthetic Psychological Safety and Team Learning Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Psychological Safety, Voice Risk, and Team Performance Integrity Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, psychological safety review, leadership and voice-system education, team learning analysis, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- courage scoring
- worker voice scoring
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real executives, real teams, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- psychological safety scores
- safety risk scores
- strong team performance predictions
- symbolic voice-risk summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as team and institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker and stakeholder consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex psychological safety phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure power, status, trust, fear, retaliation memory, workload, meeting design, and lived experience.
- Psychological safety cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
