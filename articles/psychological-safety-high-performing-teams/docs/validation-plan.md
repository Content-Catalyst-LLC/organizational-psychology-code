# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, surveillance, courage-scoring, voice-scoring, performance-ranking, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent team-system and institutional conditions rather than individual worker traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe a team climate or institutional condition?
- Does it describe leadership openness, inclusion quality, learning review quality, blame intensity, status asymmetry, or perceived penalty?
- Does it describe the environment rather than an individual's courage or attitude?
- Could it be misused to label or rank individuals?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- fear of retaliation
- underreporting of concerns
- leadership self-report bias
- uneven safety across status groups
- aggregate scores hiding marginalization
- meeting participation patterns
- psychological safety survey confidentiality
- context behind numeric indicators

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match team-review evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, loyalty scoring, courage scoring, voice scoring, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

A psychological safety review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- stronger leadership openness
- lower blame intensity
- lower perceived penalty
- stronger inclusion quality
- better learning reviews
- safer upward voice
- better error-reporting practices
- more credible concern follow-through
