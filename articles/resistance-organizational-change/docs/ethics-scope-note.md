# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, organizational change governance, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, work system, change process, implementation environment, governance structure, or institutional condition.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as resistant, nonresistant, adaptable, unadaptable, loyal, disloyal, high-risk, suitable, unsuitable, resilient, fragile, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Resistance signals may reflect structural underinvestment, unclear authority, poor sequencing, low trust, excessive workload, weak implementation support, threatened identity, power redistribution, or prior failed reforms.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Change analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not worker exposure. If a workflow causes employees to fear raising concerns, questioning implementation, or reporting risks, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible change.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
