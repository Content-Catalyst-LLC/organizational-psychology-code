# Strategic Decision-Making in Complex Organizations

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Strategic Decision-Making in Complex Organizations.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying strategic decision-making as an institutional process shaped by uncertainty, bounded rationality, distributed knowledge, power, governance, bias, learning, and strategic resilience.

The scaffold treats strategic decision-making not as a purely technical planning exercise, but as a system-level process involving environmental sensing, interpretive coherence, knowledge integration, option diversity, governance discipline, adaptive learning, bias pressure, political distortion, complexity load, and delayed strategic outcomes.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- strategic decision-process research scaffolding
- institutional strategy review
- governance and decision-quality diagnostics
- strategic risk and resilience teaching
- scenario analysis under uncertainty
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee scoring, workplace surveillance, psychological assessment, executive ranking, or employment decision-making.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models strategic decision quality as a system-level construct shaped by:

- environmental sensing quality
- interpretive coherence
- knowledge integration
- option diversity
- governance quality
- adaptive learning
- bias pressure
- political distortion
- complexity load
- environmental turbulence
- strategic success
- strategic risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, strategic governance, decision-process review, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, psychological assessment tools, or executive ranking systems.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the institution, strategic decision process, governance structure, business unit, strategic review system, or organizational condition—not the worth, intelligence, loyalty, adaptability, competence, employability, or psychological status of an individual worker or leader.

## Suggested workflow

1. Read `docs/ethics-scope-note.md`.
2. Review `docs/research-design.md`.
3. Inspect `docs/data-dictionary.md`.
4. Run the Python workflow in `python/strategic_decision_quality_model.py`.
5. Run the R workflow in `r/strategic_decision_quality_model.R`.
6. Use `sql/schema.sql` for a reproducible relational representation.
7. Review `docs/validation-plan.md` and `docs/model-card.md`.
8. Use the WordPress GitHub embed block in `github-embed-wordpress.html`.
