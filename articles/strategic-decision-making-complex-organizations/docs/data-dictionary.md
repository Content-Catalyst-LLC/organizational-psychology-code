# Data Dictionary

## `data/raw/strategic_decision_quality_synthetic.csv`

This synthetic dataset describes organizational units across repeated strategic decision periods. It is designed for methods demonstration only.

| Field | Description | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| `unit_id` | Synthetic organizational unit identifier | Categorical |
| `period` | Synthetic observation period | Integer |
| `sensing_quality` | Ability to detect environmental, stakeholder, regulatory, market, and operational signals | 0–100 |
| `interpretive_coherence` | Degree of shared strategic sensemaking across relevant decision-makers | 0–100 |
| `knowledge_integration` | Ability to integrate distributed knowledge across functions, roles, and units | 0–100 |
| `option_diversity` | Breadth and quality of strategic alternatives considered | 0–100 |
| `governance_quality` | Rigor of strategic review, challenge, authorization, and accountability | 0–100 |
| `adaptive_learning` | Ability to revise assumptions and commitments in light of evidence | 0–100 |
| `bias_pressure` | Pressure from overconfidence, anchoring, confirmation bias, escalation, or identity protection | 0–100 |
| `political_distortion` | Degree to which coalitions, agenda control, status protection, or resource politics distort strategic reasoning | 0–100 |
| `complexity_load` | Complexity of the strategic environment, task, or institutional field | 0–100 |
| `environmental_turbulence` | Degree of volatility, uncertainty, disruption, or external change | 0–100 |
| `strategic_success` | Synthetic binary indicator of meaningful strategic success | 0/1 |
| `symbolic_strategy` | Synthetic binary indicator of strategy language or planning activity without deeper institutional quality | 0/1 |

## Interpretation

Variables are institutional, unit-level, and decision-system indicators. They should not be interpreted as measures of individual intelligence, executive competence, loyalty, attitude, performance, personality, or psychological status.
