# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, strategic governance, decision-process review, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment
- executive ranking
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the organization, strategic decision process, governance structure, business unit, strategic review system, or institutional condition.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker or leader as strategic, unstrategic, biased, politically distorted, competent, incompetent, high-risk, suitable, unsuitable, resilient, fragile, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Strategic risk signals may reflect structural underinvestment, poor information flow, weak governance, narrow option generation, silenced dissent, political filtering, distorted incentives, overconfidence, path dependence, or an overly narrow strategic frame.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Strategic analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear raising concerns, challenging assumptions, or reporting strategic risks, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible strategy.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
