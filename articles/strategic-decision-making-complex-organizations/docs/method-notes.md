# Method Notes

This scaffold demonstrates how strategic decision quality can be modeled while preserving interpretive caution.

## Conceptual model

The model treats strategic decision quality as an interaction among:

- environmental sensing quality
- interpretive coherence
- knowledge integration
- option diversity
- governance quality
- adaptive learning
- bias pressure
- political distortion
- complexity load
- environmental turbulence

## Suggested methods

- descriptive diagnostics
- unit-level strategic review dashboards
- logistic regression for strategic success
- symbolic strategy risk review
- strategic risk scoring
- scenario analysis
- sensitivity analysis
- qualitative validation
- governance review

## Methodological caution

Strategic decision-making is partly measurable but not fully captured by metrics. It involves meaning, power, identity, governance, foresight, institutional memory, delayed feedback, and uncertainty. Quantitative outputs should be paired with qualitative research and ethical governance.
