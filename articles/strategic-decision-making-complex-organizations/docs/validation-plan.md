# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, executive-ranking, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent strategic decision-system conditions rather than individual worker or executive traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe the strategic process?
- Does it describe governance, sensing, integration, or learning?
- Does it describe institutional decision conditions?
- Could it be misused to rank or label individuals?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- missing decision records
- political filtering
- underreporting of dissent
- retrospective rationalization
- uneven participation
- changes in measurement over time
- context behind numeric indicators

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match strategic review evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, executive ranking, punishment, compensation decisions, promotion decisions, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

A strategic review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- better environmental sensing
- broader option generation
- stronger challenge norms
- improved governance
- better decision documentation
- clearer kill criteria
- better postmortem learning
- reduced symbolic strategy
