# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, team-system review, collaboration-system education, communication and coordination analysis, leadership and team-design review, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- team-fit scoring
- collaboration scoring of workers
- worker-risk scoring
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the team environment, collaboration system, meeting system, communication architecture, leadership routine, role system, decision process, governance structure, cross-functional relationship, organizational unit, or institutional context.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as cooperative, uncooperative, high-performing, low-performing, difficult, safe, unsafe, loyal, disloyal, resistant, nonresistant, high-risk, suitable, unsuitable, positive, problematic, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Team-dynamics risk signals may reflect structural contradictions, unclear authority, weak role design, unreliable communication, poor leadership support, high coordination cost, task ambiguity, unsafe voice conditions, hidden coordination labor, participation inequity, workload pressure, or weak learning routines.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Team analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear naming communication problems, distrust, ambiguity, role confusion, conflict, workload strain, leadership behavior, retaliation, or lived team experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible team review.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
