# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and team-dynamics review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Communication

- Are decisions, assumptions, risks, and handoffs documented clearly?
- Is communication timely, interpretable, and actionable?
- Does high communication volume hide poor coordination?

## Role clarity

- Do members know who owns responsibilities and decisions?
- Are handoffs and dependencies explicit?
- Are authority gaps creating hidden conflict?

## Psychological safety

- Can members ask questions, raise concerns, report errors, and disagree safely?
- What happened the last time someone named a problem?
- Is silence being misread as agreement?

## Trust and cooperation

- Do members rely on one another without excessive defensive monitoring?
- Are burdens and support shared fairly?
- Is cooperation one-sided or reciprocal?

## Conflict process

- Is conflict task-focused, process-focused, relationship-focused, structural, or value-based?
- Are conflicts resolved, avoided, personalized, or learned from?
- Are repeated conflicts triggering structural review?

## Participation equity

- Who speaks, whose ideas are adopted, and who receives credit?
- Who carries hidden coordination or repair labor?
- Who pays the highest cost for dissent?

## Learning routines

- Do retrospectives, postmortems, and reviews change practice?
- Are lessons documented and revisited?
- Does the team build future capacity or consume it?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and governance review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, collaboration scorecard, team-fit scorecard, loyalty scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
