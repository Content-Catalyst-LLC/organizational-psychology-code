# Method Notes

This scaffold demonstrates how team effectiveness, team-system risk, and strong collective performance can be modeled while preserving interpretive caution.

## Conceptual model

The model treats team effectiveness as an interaction among:

- communication quality
- trust level
- role clarity
- psychological safety
- leadership support
- participation equity
- learning routine quality
- conflict load
- task ambiguity
- coordination cost

## Suggested methods

- descriptive diagnostics
- team-level dashboards
- logistic regression for strong collective performance
- symbolic teamwork-risk review
- team-system risk scoring
- scenario analysis
- sensitivity analysis
- qualitative validation
- meeting and communication audit
- role mapping and decision-right review
- retrospective and learning-routine review

## Methodological caution

Team dynamics are partly measurable but not fully captured by metrics. They involve meaning, history, power, trust, safety, workload, authority, role clarity, meeting design, leadership behavior, communication architecture, and lived experience. Quantitative outputs should be paired with qualitative research and ethical governance.
