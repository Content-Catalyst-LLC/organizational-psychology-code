# Model Card: Synthetic Team Dynamics and Strong Collective Performance Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Team Effectiveness, Team-System Risk, and Strong Collective Performance Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, team-system review, collaboration-system education, communication and coordination analysis, leadership and team-design review, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- team-fit scoring
- collaboration scoring of workers
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real teams, real workplaces, real leaders, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- team effectiveness scores
- team-system risk scores
- strong collective performance predictions
- symbolic teamwork-risk summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as team and institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker and stakeholder consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex team-dynamics phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure power, history, retaliation risk, hidden labor, unequal participation, weak authority design, workload, and lived experience.
- Team dynamics cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
