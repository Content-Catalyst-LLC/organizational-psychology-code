# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, surveillance, collaboration-scoring, team-fit scoring, performance-ranking, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent team-system and institutional conditions rather than individual worker traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe a team process, communication pattern, role system, or institutional condition?
- Does it describe communication, trust, role clarity, psychological safety, leadership support, conflict load, ambiguity, coordination cost, participation equity, or learning routines?
- Does it describe the environment rather than an individual's personality, loyalty, or productivity?
- Could it be misused to label or rank individuals?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- fear of retaliation
- underreporting of conflict or distrust
- meeting observation bias
- aggregate scores hiding marginalized experience
- hidden coordination labor
- uneven participation or credit
- role ambiguity and decision-right gaps
- context behind numeric indicators

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match team-review evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, loyalty scoring, team-fit scoring, collaboration scoring, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

A team-dynamics review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- stronger communication architecture
- clearer roles and decision rights
- safer voice and early warning
- lower coordination cost
- better conflict process
- fairer participation and credit
- stronger learning routines
- better leadership support
