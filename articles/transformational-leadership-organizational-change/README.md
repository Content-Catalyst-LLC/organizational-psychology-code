# Transformational Leadership and Organizational Change

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Transformational Leadership and Organizational Change.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying transformational leadership as a system-level condition shaped by vision credibility, motivational alignment, intellectual stimulation, developmental support, institutional trust, psychological safety, change communication, implementation support, resistance intensity, ambiguity pressure, change fatigue, voice-suppression risk, and adaptive performance.

The scaffold treats transformational leadership as a leadership-system and change-system construct. It is not a charisma score, employee loyalty score, individual motivation score, dissent-tracking system, or psychological assessment tool. It supports institutional learning about how leaders make change meaningful, credible, participatory, developmental, ethical, and operationally usable.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- transformational-leadership and change-system review
- vision credibility and meaning-making analysis
- institutional trust and psychological safety diagnostics
- intellectual stimulation and organizational learning review
- developmental support and implementation support analysis
- change fatigue, resistance intensity, and ambiguity-pressure review
- adaptive performance and innovation simulation
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee screening, employee ranking, worker motivation scoring, leadership-loyalty scoring, dissent tracking, psychological assessment, workplace surveillance, employment decision-making, or worker risk labeling.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models transformational capacity and adaptive performance as system-level constructs shaped by:

- vision credibility

- institutional trust
- psychological safety
- change communication
- implementation support
- meaning-making quality
- resistance intensity

- adaptive performance risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, transformational-leadership review, change-system analysis, vision/trust/support/fatigue diagnostics, psychological-safety and organizational-learning education, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, productivity-ranking tools, leadership-loyalty scoring tools, dissent-tracking tools, psychological assessment tools, or automated personnel decision-making systems.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the leadership system, change system, work system, team environment, communication routine, psychological-safety climate, organizational unit, change-governance process, or institutional context—not the worth, productivity, loyalty, motivation, morality, psychological status, employability, or legitimacy of any individual worker.

## Suggested workflow

1. Read `docs/ethics-scope-note.md`.
2. Review `docs/research-design.md`.
3. Inspect `docs/data-dictionary.md`.
