# Data Dictionary

## `data/raw/transformational_leadership_synthetic.csv`

This synthetic dataset describes organizational units across repeated transformational-leadership and change-system review periods. It is designed for methods demonstration only.

| Field | Description | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| `unit_id` | Synthetic organizational unit identifier | Categorical |
| `period` | Synthetic observation period | Integer |
| `vision_credibility` | Degree to which the change vision is coherent, believable, evidence-informed, and meaningful | 0–100 |
| `motivational_alignment` | Degree to which individual and collective motivation align with shared change goals | 0–100 |
| `intellectual_stimulation` | Degree to which leaders invite questioning, experimentation, and new thinking | 0–100 |
| `developmental_support` | Degree to which employees are equipped, coached, and supported for new demands | 0–100 |
| `institutional_trust` | Degree to which leadership has earned credibility, consistency, and legitimacy | 0–100 |
| `psychological_safety` | Degree to which people can speak honestly, challenge assumptions, and raise concerns without retaliation | 0–100 |
| `change_communication` | Clarity, frequency, usefulness, and honesty of communication during transformation | 0–100 |
| `implementation_support` | Resources, staffing, tools, time, and operational support for change | 0–100 |
| `meaning_making_quality` | Degree to which leadership helps people interpret why change matters and how their work connects | 0–100 |
| `resistance_intensity` | Degree of skepticism, active resistance, interpretive rejection, or noncooperation | 0–100 |
| `ambiguity_pressure` | Degree of confusion about purpose, goals, roles, priorities, or decision rights | 0–100 |
| `change_fatigue` | Degree of exhaustion, saturation, overload, or change weariness | 0–100 |
| `voice_suppression_risk` | Synthetic binary indicator of elevated risk that employees will withhold concerns or dissent | 0/1 |
| `adaptive_performance_risk` | Synthetic binary indicator of elevated risk to adaptive performance under change | 0/1 |
| `style_misfit_risk` | Synthetic binary indicator that leadership style appears poorly matched to transformation context | 0/1 |
| `purpose_exploitation_risk` | Synthetic binary indicator that purpose language may be used to normalize sacrifice or overwork | 0/1 |

## Interpretation

Variables are unit-level, leadership-system-level, change-system-level, and institutional indicators. They should not be interpreted as measures of individual worker productivity, loyalty, motivation, dissent, employability, morality, psychological status, or worth.
