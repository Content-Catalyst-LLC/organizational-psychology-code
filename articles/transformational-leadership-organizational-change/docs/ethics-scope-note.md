# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, transformational-leadership review, change-system analysis, vision/trust/support/fatigue diagnostics, psychological-safety and organizational-learning education, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- leadership-loyalty scoring
- worker-risk scoring
- dissent tracking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the leadership system, change system, work system, team environment, communication routine, psychological-safety climate, organizational unit, change-governance process, or institutional context.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as motivated, unmotivated, loyal, disloyal, productive, unproductive, dissenting, compliant, noncompliant, high-risk, low-risk, resistant, suitable, unsuitable, psychologically deficient, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Transformational capacity and adaptive-performance risk signals may reflect unclear vision, low trust, weak psychological safety, insufficient support, excessive change fatigue, ambiguity, resistance intensity, implementation gaps, or purpose language being used in ways that normalize sacrifice.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Transformational-leadership analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear naming weak vision, distrust, fatigue, ambiguity, voice suppression, purpose exploitation, or lived work experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible change review.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
