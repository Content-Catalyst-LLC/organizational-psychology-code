# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and transformational-leadership review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Vision and meaning

- Is the change vision credible, coherent, and connected to real constraints?
- Do employees understand why transformation matters?
- Is vision being used to clarify purpose or to pressure compliance?

## Trust and legitimacy

- Has leadership earned belief through consistency and follow-through?
- Are tradeoffs and uncertainties acknowledged honestly?
- Are decision processes procedurally fair?

## Intellectual stimulation

- Can employees question assumptions safely?
- Are weak signals, concerns, and dissent treated as learning resources?
- Are ideas connected to implementation pathways?

## Developmental support

- Are people equipped for new demands?
- Is training matched with time, resources, staffing, and managerial support?
- Are development opportunities distributed fairly?

## Change fatigue and ambiguity

- Is the organization monitoring overload and exhaustion?
- Are priorities clear enough to reduce ambiguity?
- Is change paced responsibly?

## Psychological safety and voice

- Can lower-power employees raise concerns without retaliation?
- Is skepticism treated as evidence about conditions, not attitude failure?
- Are employees protected when they name contradictions between vision and practice?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and transformational-leadership review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, dissent-tracking system, loyalty scorecard, productivity scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
