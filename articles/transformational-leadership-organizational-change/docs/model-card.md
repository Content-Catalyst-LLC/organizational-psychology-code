# Model Card: Synthetic Transformational Leadership and Adaptive Change Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Transformational Leadership, Vision Credibility, Trust, Change Fatigue, and Adaptive Performance Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, transformational-leadership review, change-system analysis, vision/trust/support/fatigue diagnostics, psychological-safety and organizational-learning education, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- productivity ranking
- leadership-loyalty scoring
- dissent tracking
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real leaders, real units, real teams, real workplaces, real change programs, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- transformational capacity scores
- transformational system risk scores
- adaptive performance risk summaries
- voice-suppression risk summaries
- purpose-exploitation risk summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as unit and institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker and stakeholder consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex leadership, culture, power, fatigue, and performance phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure history, retaliation risk, hidden labor, role hierarchy, status differences, resource constraints, change burden, and lived experience.
- Transformational leadership cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
