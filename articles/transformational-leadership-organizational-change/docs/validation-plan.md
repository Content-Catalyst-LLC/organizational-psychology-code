# Validation Plan

This validation plan applies to synthetic methods demonstration. It does not validate a real-world employment, personnel, surveillance, dissent-tracking, productivity-ranking, leadership-loyalty scoring, or psychological assessment tool.

## 1. Construct validation

Review whether variables represent leadership-system and change-system conditions rather than individual worker traits.

Key questions:

- Does the measure describe vision credibility, meaning-making, communication, trust, psychological safety, support, fatigue, ambiguity, or institutional context?
- Does it describe the change environment rather than an individual worker's loyalty, motivation, positivity, dissent, productivity, or psychological status?
- Could it be misused to identify critics, punish employee skepticism, rank workers, or surveil team sentiment?
- Does the measure support institutional learning rather than individual blame?

## 2. Data quality review

In real-world research, examine:

- source provenance
- fear of retaliation
- self-censorship and silence
- change fatigue and overload
- unequal exposure to change burden
- leadership credibility and follow-through
- ambiguity in roles and decision rights
- implementation support and resource availability
- context behind numeric indicators

## 3. Model validation

Assess:

- coefficient stability
- sensitivity to weighting choices
- uncertainty
- outlier influence
- subgroup interpretability
- qualitative plausibility
- whether model outputs match change-review evidence

## 4. Ethical validation

Confirm that the workflow cannot reasonably be repurposed for individual worker monitoring, dissent tracking, loyalty scoring, productivity ranking, discipline, promotion decisions, compensation decisions, termination, or employment decisions.

## 5. Action validation

A transformational-leadership review is only useful if it leads to responsible institutional improvement. Track whether findings lead to:

- clearer vision and purpose
- stronger trust
- better change communication
- safer voice channels
- stronger psychological safety
- better developmental support
- reduced ambiguity
- reduced change fatigue
- improved implementation support
- ethical use of purpose language
