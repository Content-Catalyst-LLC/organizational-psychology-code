# Trust and Cooperation in Workplace Teams

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Trust and Cooperation in Workplace Teams.”** It provides synthetic datasets, reproducible workflows, documentation, validation notes, and multi-language examples for studying trust and cooperation as team-level and institutional conditions shaped by competence trust, integrity trust, benevolence trust, reciprocity norms, communication reliability, opportunism risk, power imbalance, uncertainty load, leadership credibility, institutional trust, and collaborative outcomes.

The scaffold treats trust and cooperation not as sentimental workplace virtues, personality traits, loyalty measures, worker trustworthiness scores, or generic teamwork rhetoric. It treats them as relational and institutional infrastructures that make coordinated action, knowledge sharing, vulnerability, reciprocity, and interdependent performance possible under uncertainty.

## Research purpose

This repository is designed for:

- organizational psychology methods demonstration
- synthetic-data modeling
- trust and cooperation diagnostics
- reciprocity and mutual-support analysis
- communication reliability review
- institutional trust and procedural fairness education
- opportunism risk and power imbalance review
- collaboration system and cross-boundary coordination analysis
- leadership credibility and governance review
- responsible and reproducible organizational analytics

It is not designed for employee screening, cultural-loyalty scoring, trustworthiness scoring of workers, cooperation scoring of workers, psychological assessment, productivity ranking, workplace surveillance, employment decision-making, or individual risk labeling.

## Conceptual frame

The synthetic workflow models cooperative capacity and strong collaboration as system-level constructs shaped by:

- competence trust
- integrity trust
- benevolence trust
- reciprocity norms
- communication reliability
- opportunism risk
- power imbalance
- uncertainty load
- leadership credibility
- institutional trust
- strong collaboration
- symbolic cooperation risk

## Responsible-use notice

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, trust-and-cooperation review, collaboration-system education, reciprocity analysis, leadership and governance review, and reproducible workflows.

They are **not** employee-screening tools, employment-selection tools, hiring tools, promotion tools, compensation tools, discipline tools, termination tools, workplace surveillance systems, individual performance-management systems, cultural-loyalty scoring tools, trustworthiness scoring tools, cooperation scoring tools, productivity-ranking tools, psychological assessment tools, or automated personnel decision-making systems.

The appropriate unit of analysis is the team environment, collaboration system, governance structure, institutional trust condition, cross-functional relationship, communication system, leadership routine, work system, stakeholder relationship, or organizational unit—not the worth, loyalty, trustworthiness, cooperativeness, personality, employability, psychological status, morality, or legitimacy of any individual worker.

## Suggested workflow

1. Read `docs/ethics-scope-note.md`.
2. Review `docs/research-design.md`.
