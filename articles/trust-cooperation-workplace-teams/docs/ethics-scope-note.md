# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, trust-and-cooperation review, collaboration-system education, reciprocity analysis, leadership and governance review, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- trustworthiness scoring
- cooperation scoring of workers
- worker-risk scoring
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the team environment, collaboration system, governance structure, institutional trust condition, cross-functional relationship, communication system, leadership routine, work system, stakeholder relationship, or organizational unit.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as trustworthy, untrustworthy, cooperative, uncooperative, loyal, disloyal, resistant, nonresistant, high-risk, suitable, unsuitable, positive, difficult, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Trust-and-cooperation risk signals may reflect structural contradictions, unclear authority, weak institutional trust, unreliable communication, opportunistic incentives, unequal burden-sharing, low leadership credibility, power imbalance, retaliation memory, workload pressure, or weak reciprocity norms.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Trust analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear naming distrust, unfairness, opportunism, burden imbalance, retaliation, or lived team experience, it is undermining the learning conditions needed for responsible trust review.

## Governance requirement

Any real-world adaptation should include:

- informed governance
- worker and stakeholder consultation
- privacy review
- anti-retaliation safeguards
- transparency about purpose
- limits on use
- human interpretation
- rights-preserving oversight
