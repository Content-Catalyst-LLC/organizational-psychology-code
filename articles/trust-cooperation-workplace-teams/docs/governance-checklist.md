# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and trust-and-cooperation review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Competence trust

- Are roles clear enough for reliable coordination?
- Do teams have the capability and resources needed to honor commitments?
- Are errors addressed through learning rather than repeated unmanaged failure?

## Integrity trust

- Are commitments tracked and honored?
- Are decisions explained with visible criteria and constraints?
- Do leaders and high-status actors follow the same standards expected of others?

## Benevolence trust

- Is vulnerability handled with care?
- Are people protected after asking for help, reporting risk, or naming uncertainty?
- Are mistakes and disclosures used responsibly?

## Reciprocity

- Are burdens, help, support labor, and recognition distributed fairly?
- Who repeatedly absorbs extra work so cooperation appears smooth?
- Are mutual support norms credible or one-sided?

## Communication reliability

- Are commitments, handoffs, decisions, and dependencies visible?
- Are delays and silence interpreted with enough context?
- Do distributed teams have shared communication norms?

## Opportunism and power

- Do incentives reward information hoarding or local optimization?
- Can lower-power groups trust processes without absorbing greater risk?
- Does cooperation become compliance under pressure?

## Institutional trust

- Are procedures fair, explainable, and consistently applied?
- Are breaches acknowledged and repaired?
- Does the organization make trust reasonable through governance rather than demanded through rhetoric?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and governance review. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, trustworthiness scorecard, cooperation scorecard, loyalty scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
