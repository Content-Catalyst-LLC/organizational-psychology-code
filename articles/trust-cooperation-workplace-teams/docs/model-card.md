# Model Card: Synthetic Trust, Cooperation, and Strong Collaboration Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Cooperative Capacity, Trust Risk, and Strong Collaboration Workflow

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, trust-and-cooperation review, collaboration-system education, reciprocity analysis, leadership and governance review, and reproducible organizational psychology examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- cultural-loyalty scoring
- trustworthiness scoring
- cooperation scoring of workers
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real employees, real teams, real workplaces, real leaders, real stakeholders, or real organizations.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- cooperative capacity scores
- trust risk scores
- strong collaboration predictions
- symbolic cooperation-risk summaries
- review priority categories
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as team and institutional inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world use would require domain expertise, qualitative validation, ethics review, worker and stakeholder consultation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot validate real-world causal claims.
- Variables simplify complex trust and cooperation phenomena.
- Quantitative scores may obscure power, history, retaliation risk, hidden labor, unequal burden, trust repair, procedural legitimacy, workload, and lived experience.
- Trust and cooperation cannot be fully represented through metrics.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
