# Data Dictionary

## `data/raw/organizational_psychology_synthetic.csv`

This synthetic dataset describes people nested in teams. It is designed for methods demonstration only.

| Field | Description | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| `team_id` | Synthetic team identifier | Categorical |
| `person_id` | Synthetic person identifier within a team | Categorical |
| `capability` | Task-relevant competence or capacity under current work conditions | 0–100 |
| `motivation` | Energy, engagement, and willingness to invest effort | 0–100 |
| `role_clarity` | Clarity of responsibilities, goals, decision rights, and expectations | 0–100 |
| `autonomy` | Degree of appropriate discretion and self-direction | 0–100 |
| `fairness` | Perceived fairness of procedures, expectations, recognition, and treatment | 0–100 |
| `team_coordination` | Quality of coordination, interdependence management, and shared work routines | 0–100 |
| `leadership_trust` | Credibility, consistency, and trustworthiness of leadership | 0–100 |
| `psychological_safety` | Ability to ask questions, raise concerns, admit uncertainty, and speak honestly | 0–100 |
| `communication_quality` | Clarity, timeliness, openness, and usefulness of information flow | 0–100 |
| `culture_strength` | Coherence and credibility of shared norms and expectations | 0–100 |
| `workload_pressure` | Degree of demand, time pressure, strain, and work intensity | 0–100 |
| `burnout_pressure` | Degree of exhaustion risk, depletion, cynicism, or sustained strain | 0–100 |
| `institutional_support` | Availability of resources, support, staffing, tools, and institutional backing | 0–100 |
| `voice_access` | Degree to which people can raise concerns and influence relevant decisions | 0–100 |
| `outcome_quality` | Synthetic outcome quality score | 0–100 |
| `work_system_risk` | Synthetic system-level risk score | 0–100 |

## Interpretation

Variables are demonstration constructs for organizational psychology education. They are not real assessments and must not be used to judge individual employees, screen candidates, rank workers, track dissent, or automate employment decisions.
