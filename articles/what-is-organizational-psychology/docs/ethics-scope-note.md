# Ethics and Scope Note

This scaffold is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, institutional learning, organizational psychology education, work-system analysis, leadership/team/culture/motivation/psychological-safety review, and reproducible workflows.

It is not designed, validated, or appropriate for:

- employee screening
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- productivity ranking
- loyalty scoring
- dissent tracking
- psychological assessment
- automated personnel decision-making

## Appropriate unit of analysis

The appropriate unit of analysis is the work system, team environment, organizational unit, institutional context, communication routine, job-design condition, psychological-safety climate, or organizational learning process.

The workflow should never be used to label an individual worker as productive, unproductive, loyal, disloyal, motivated, unmotivated, dissenting, compliant, psychologically deficient, high-risk, low-risk, suitable, unsuitable, or employable.

## Responsible interpretation

Work-system signals may reflect unclear roles, weak leadership trust, poor communication, excessive workload, inadequate support, low psychological safety, cultural contradiction, or institutional constraints.

## Anti-surveillance principle

Organizational psychology analytics should increase institutional responsibility, not individual exposure. If a workflow causes people to fear naming workload, distrust, role ambiguity, weak leadership, burnout, unfairness, or unsafe voice conditions, it is undermining responsible organizational learning.
