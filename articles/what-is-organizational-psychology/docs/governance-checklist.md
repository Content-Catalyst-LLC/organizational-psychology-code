# Governance Checklist

Use this checklist for institutional learning and organizational psychology review. Do not use it to rate, rank, screen, discipline, surveil, or assess individual workers.

## Work design

- Are roles, goals, responsibilities, and decision rights clear?
- Is autonomy appropriate to the work and risk level?
- Are workload and staffing sustainable?

## Motivation and fairness

- Are people motivated through meaningful work, recognition, autonomy, and fairness?
- Are incentives aligned with quality, ethics, learning, and cooperation?
- Are burdens and opportunities distributed fairly?

## Leadership and culture

- Is leadership trusted?
- Are values enacted in behavior and incentives?
- Do leaders protect voice and respond constructively to concerns?

## Teams and coordination

- Do teams have shared understanding and workable communication routines?
- Are conflict and disagreement handled constructively?
- Is psychological safety strong enough to support learning?

## Responsible-use reminder

This checklist supports institutional learning and responsible organizational development. It is not a personnel assessment, surveillance tool, loyalty scorecard, dissent-tracking system, productivity scorecard, or individual ranking framework.
