# Method Notes

This scaffold demonstrates a systems-oriented approach to organizational psychology.

## Conceptual model

Organizational outcome quality is modeled as a function of capability, motivation, role clarity, autonomy, fairness, team coordination, leadership trust, psychological safety, communication quality, culture strength, institutional support, voice access, workload pressure, and burnout pressure.

## Suggested methods

- descriptive team summaries
- multilevel modeling
- scenario analysis
- regression modeling
- work-system risk review
- psychological safety and leadership trust review
- workload and burnout-pressure review
- qualitative validation

## Methodological caution

Synthetic data cannot validate real-world conclusions. Real organizational psychology research requires careful design, ethical review, validated measures, attention to power and privacy, and interpretive humility.
