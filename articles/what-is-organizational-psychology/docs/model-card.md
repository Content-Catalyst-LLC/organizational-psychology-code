# Model Card: Synthetic Organizational Psychology Work-System Workflow

## Model name

Synthetic Organizational Psychology Work-System Model

## Intended use

This workflow is intended for synthetic-data demonstration, research-methods teaching, institutional learning, organizational psychology education, and reproducible examples.

## Not intended for

- employee screening
- employee ranking
- productivity ranking
- loyalty scoring
- dissent tracking
- employment selection
- hiring
- promotion
- compensation
- discipline
- termination
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance management
- psychological assessment

## Data

The included dataset is synthetic. It does not describe real people, real teams, real organizations, or real institutions.

## Outputs

The workflow can produce:

- outcome quality summaries
- work-system risk summaries
- team-level review priorities
- model coefficients
- scenario comparisons

## Interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as work-system inquiry prompts, not automated decisions. Any real-world adaptation would require domain expertise, validated measurement, qualitative validation, privacy review, and governance oversight.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic data cannot support real-world causal claims.
- Human work behavior is simplified into demonstration variables.
- Quantitative summaries can obscure lived experience, power, history, and inequality.
- Model outputs may be misused without strong governance.
