# Research Design

This scaffold supports methods demonstration for studying organizational psychology as a multilevel work-system field.

## Research question

How can organizational psychology concepts such as capability, motivation, role clarity, autonomy, fairness, team coordination, leadership trust, psychological safety, communication quality, culture strength, workload pressure, burnout pressure, institutional support, voice access, outcome quality, and work-system risk be represented, simulated, and reviewed as individual, team, and organizational constructs?

## Conceptual assumptions

1. Work behavior is shaped by individual, team, and organizational conditions.
2. Outcomes should not be attributed to individual effort alone.
3. Motivation, role clarity, trust, safety, communication, workload, and culture interact.
4. Work-system risk is a system-level condition, not a worker-defect score.
5. Organizational psychology analytics must support institutional learning and responsible work-system improvement.

## Synthetic modeling aims

The scaffold demonstrates how to:

- create synthetic organizational psychology data
- summarize work-system conditions by team
- model outcome quality from individual and system variables
- compare high-trust and high-risk work-system scenarios
- document ethical boundaries and responsible-use constraints

## Appropriate interpretation

Outputs should be interpreted as prompts for institutional inquiry. A high-risk synthetic team profile should never be read as a real-world finding without qualitative validation, participatory review, privacy safeguards, and attention to workload, role design, leadership behavior, culture, and power.
